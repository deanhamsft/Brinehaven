Sea Hag Lair: The Murkmoor Den

Overview

- Location: Nestled deep within the salt marshes 10 miles north of Brinehaven, the Murkmoor Den is a sunken cave complex accessible via a brackish pool. The marshes, soggy and twisted with mangroves, hide the lair’s entrance, marked by a faint stench of decay and glowing algae. The sea hag, Morgra the Mirewitch, has allied with the sahuagin priestess to exploit the black tarry reagent, enhancing her curses and guarding a stash linked to Retch’s smuggling.
- Theme: A grotesque underwater lair filled with traps, minions, and magical corruption, reflecting the hag’s malevolent nature and the reagent’s influence.
- Level Suitability: Designed for a Level 4-5 party (CR 2-3 encounters, totaling 1,200-1,600 XP for 4-6 players).
- Time: Exploration takes 2-3 hours..

Dungeon Layout

The lair consists of five chambers, connected by flooded tunnels (5 ft wide, 5 ft deep, swim speed required or DC 10 Strength (Athletics)). Each chamber is 20x20 ft unless noted, with 10-15 ft ceilings. The map is a looping layout with a central sanctum.

**Chamber 1: Brackish Entry**

- Description: **A murky pool leads into the lair, its surface rippling with bioluminescent algae. Skeletal fish float amid the reeds, and a rickety wooden platform offers a precarious foothold.**
- Inhabitants: 2 Sahuagin (CR 1/2, Monster Manual p. 263), guarding the entrance with spears.
- Features: A submerged pressure plate (DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) to spot, DC 12 Dexterity save or 2d6 bludgeoning from falling logs, 10-ft radius).
- Challenge: Combat (100 XP total) or Stealth (DC 13) to bypass. A DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) on the algae reveals a reagent trace.
- Loot: 10 gp in a waterproof pouch.

**Chamber 2: Mire Trap Room**

- Description: **A flooded chamber with sticky mud walls and a ceiling dripping with black tarry residue. A rickety bridge spans a 10-ft-deep pit of acidic sludge.**
- Inhabitants: 1 Giant Toad (CR 1, Monster Manual p. 329), mutated by the reagent, lurking in the pit.
- Features: The bridge (DC 12 Dexterity (Acrobatics) or fall into 2d6 acid damage, DC 13 Constitution save for half). A hidden ledge (DC 14 Wisdom (Perception)) holds a vial of reagent.
- Challenge: Combat (200 XP) or Agility (DC 14 Strength (Athletics) to jump the pit). The toad’s tongue grapple (DC 13 Strength save) adds risk.
- Loot: 1 vial of black tarry reagent, 15 gp in a mud-caked chest.

**Chamber 3: Reagent Vat**

- Description: **A circular chamber with a bubbling vat of black tarry liquid, tended by a crude alchemical setup. The air is thick with acrid fumes.**
- Inhabitants: 1 Sahuagin Priestess (CR 2, Monster Manual p. 264), overseeing the reagent production with 1 Sahuagin assistant.
- Features: The vat (DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana)) can be sabotaged (DC 14 Dexterity check) to explode (3d6 acid damage, 15-ft radius, DC 13 Dexterity save), destroying 2d4 vials but alerting Morgra.
- Challenge: Combat (300 XP) or Sabotage (risk vs. reward). The priestess casts Spiritual Weapon (trident).
- Loot: 2d4 vials of reagent, 25 gp in coral beads.

**Chamber 4: Hag’s Lair**

- Description: **A damp chamber with a throne of bones and seaweed, adorned with mirrors reflecting distorted faces. A pool of dark water hides Morgra’s treasures.**
- Inhabitants: Morgra the Mirewitch (CR 5, Monster Manual p. 179), a sea hag with enhanced abilities (add +2 to AC and damage from reagent use).
- Features: Mirrors impose Death Glare (DC 14 Wisdom save or 2d10 psychic damage, recharge 5-6). A submerged chest (DC 13 Strength (Athletics)) holds loot.
- Challenge: Combat (1,100 XP) or Bargain (DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) to offer reagent vials for safe passage). Her Illusory Appearance complicates tactics.
- Loot: Pearl of Power, 50 gp, a hag’s eye (alerts her if taken, DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) to disable).

**Chamber 5: Reagent Cache**

- Description: **A narrow chamber with shelves of reagent vials and a map scratched into the wall, showing the Auror’s Bounty wreck.**
- Inhabitants: None, but a Water Elemental (CR 5, Monster Manual p. 125, scaled to CR 3 with 60 HP) guards if Morgra is defeated.
- Features: The map (DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)) details the wreck’s barrier, hinting at the reagent’s use. 3d6 vials are stored.
- Challenge: Combat (700 XP) or Evasion (DC 15 Dexterity (Stealth) to avoid the elemental). Success yields the cache.
- Loot: 3d6 vials, the map (plot item), 30 gp in a locked box (DC 13 Dexterity (Thieves’ Tools)).

Dungeon Flow

- Entry (Chamber 1): The party faces sahuagin, setting a combat tone.
- Progression (Chambers 2-3): Trap room and vat offer exploration and a tougher fight, building tension.
- Climax (Chambers 4-5): The hag’s lair and cache tie to the campaign, rewarding combat and lore with a moral choice (use or destroy the reagent).
- Exit: The lair floods post-combat (DC 12 Strength (Athletics) to swim out), urging haste.

Campaign Integration

- The Auror’s Bounty: Morgra’s map and reagent stash confirm Retch’s plan to claim the wreck, linking to his fiefdom ambition.
- Rune Connection: The reagent’s ward-disrupting power ties to the markers, suggesting Retch’s interference.
- Hearthstone’s Secret: Sabotaging the vat could weaken the ward’s corruption, aiding the ritual.
- Retch and Smugglers: The lair exposes Retch as the customer, shifting Drenvar and Jorin’s loyalties if discovered.
- Sahuagin Threat: The priestess’s alliance with Morgra explains their aggression, offering a chance to disrupt it.

DM Notes

- XP Total: ~2,400 XP (sahuagin 100, toad 200, priestess 300, hag 1,100, elemental 700), split for 4-6 players (400-600 XP each). Adjust for Level 4 (1,200 XP) or 5 (1,500 XP).
- Scaling: For Level 3, reduce to 1 sahuagin and no elemental. For Level 6+, add 1 Sahuagin Baron (CR 5).
- Reagent Use: The party can use vials (1 hour ward disruption) but risks addiction (DC 13 Constitution save per use, 1 level exhaustion on failure).
- Time: The 2-3 hour explore fits 05:28 PM - 08:28 PM MDT, with dusk heightening stakes.
