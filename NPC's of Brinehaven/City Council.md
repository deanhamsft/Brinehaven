1. **Councilor Liora Thaloryn (Half-Elf, Chair of the Council)**

- Race: Half-Elf
- Age: 58 (appears late-30s)
- Gender: Female
- Appearance: Tall and graceful, with silver-streaked auburn hair braided with fishing line, sea-green eyes, and a calm, commanding presence. Wears a deep-blue cloak pinned with an ancient elven brooch shaped like a gull in flight.
- Personality:
    - Traits: Diplomatic, patient, fiercely protective of tradition.
    - Ideal: “The hearthstone’s light must never fade; it is the soul of Brinehaven.”
    - Bond: Descendant of the village’s elven founders; Lirien Thaloryn is her great-aunt.
    - Flaw: Stubborn about old ways; slow to accept outsiders’ solutions.
- Abilities (Noble background, Level 3 Bard lite):
    - AC 13 (elven chain under robes), HP 18, Speed 30 ft.
    - Skills: Persuasion +5, History +4, Insight +4, Performance +5.
    - Spells (DC 13, +5): Charm Person, Healing Word, Sleep, Calm Emotions.
    - Equipment: Elven brooch (nonmagical heirloom), silver quill, council seal.
- Role: Elected chair for life. Mediates disputes, controls council purse (500 gp treasury), and oversees the hearthstone ritual schedule.
- Current Stance:
    - Believes the hearthstone is dimming because “the old songs are forgotten.”
    - Suspicious of Sheriff Drenvar’s excuses about increased patrols.
    - Will support the party if they prove the ward can be restored (DC 15 Persuasion or evidence of smuggling).
- Interaction Quote:  
    “The sea gives and the sea takes, but the hearthstone has always kept us safe. Show me you understand its song, and the council will stand with you.”

2. **Councilor Magnus Holt (Human, Trade & Harbor Master)**

- Race: Human
- Age: 44
- Gender: Male
- Appearance: Stocky, weathered, with a salt-and-pepper beard and a perpetual squint from years staring at horizons. Wears a heavy wool coat lined with ledger pockets and a brass spyglass on a chain.
- Personality:
    - Traits: Pragmatic, profit-minded, gruffly protective of “his” harbor.
    - Ideal: “A full hold and a safe dock—that’s all any sailor needs.”
    - Bond: Sheriff Drenvar is his younger brother; family loyalty blinds him to corruption.
    - Flaw: Turns a blind eye to smuggling if it keeps tariffs flowing.
- Abilities (Merchant background, Level 2 Fighter lite):
    - AC 15 (breastplate under coat), HP 16, Speed 30 ft.
    - Skills: Athletics +4, Investigation +3, Intimidation +3.
    - Equipment: Cutlass (1d6+2), spyglass, ledger of harbor fees, council seal.
- Role: Controls dock fees, ship inspections, and trade permits. Receives 10 % of all harbor revenue.
- Current Stance:
    - Secretly accepts bribes from Jorin “Barrel” Kray to ignore certain crates (the reagent shipments).
    - Worries the sahuagin raids will scare off merchants.
    - Will side against the party unless they offer a better financial deal or expose Drenvar’s betrayal of family trust (DC 16 Persuasion or hard evidence).
- Interaction Quote:  
    “I don’t care where the coin comes from, long as the docks stay busy. Convince me you’re good for business, and I’ll listen.”

3. **Councilor Vespera Ashborn (Tiefling, Warden of the Poor & Outsiders)**

- Race: Tiefling (Zariel lineage)
- Age: 31
- Gender: Female
- Appearance: Crimson skin, small curling horns polished to a gleam, golden cat-like eyes, and a long black braid. Wears simple charcoal robes with ember-orange trim and carries a staff topped with a carved flame.
- Personality:
    - Traits: Fiery, compassionate, fiercely protective of the marginalized.
    - Ideal: “No one in Brinehaven goes hungry or homeless while I draw breath.”
    - Bond: Runs the Ashborn Refuge, a shelter for orphans and castaways (including Finn Vax and Lina Drift).
    - Flaw: Distrust of authority figures; quick to suspect corruption (correctly, in Drenvar’s case).
- Abilities (Acolyte background, Level 3 Cleric lite – Grave Domain flavor):
    - AC 14 (scale mail under robes), HP 20, Speed 30 ft.
    - Skills: Medicine +4, Religion +3, Insight +4.
    - Spells (DC 13, +5): Thaumaturgy, Inflict Wounds, Spiritual Weapon, Revivify (1/long rest via refuge funds).
    - Channel Divinity (1/rest): Turn Undead or Path to the Grave.
    - Equipment: Flame-topped staff (quarterstaff), holy symbol (a cracked hearthstone shard), council seal.
- Role: Distributes alms, oversees the refuge, and speaks for outsiders. Has veto power on banishments.
- Current Stance:
    - Already investigating strange crates disappearing from the docks (suspects Drenvar).
    - Will ally with the party instantly if they bring evidence of the reagent smuggling or harm to children.
    - Wants the hearthstone ritual opened to all villagers, not just elders.
- Interaction Quote:  
    “I’ve seen too many ‘lawmen’ line their pockets while children starve. Show me you’re different, and the refuge—and I—will stand with you.”

Council Dynamics (Quick Reference)

- Liora (Chair) – Tradition, hearthstone, slow to change.
- Magnus – Trade, family loyalty to Drenvar, bribe-able.
- Vespera – Compassion, anti-corruption, instant ally if party fights smuggling.
