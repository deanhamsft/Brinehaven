Brinehaven Commoners Smitten with Tabaxi Party Members

[[Torvyn's quest]]

**Torvyn "Fishhook" Marren**

- Race: Human
- Age: 29
- Gender: Male
- Occupation: Fisherman
- Appearance: A lean young man with sun-bleached hair tied in a messy ponytail, freckled skin, and calloused hands. His green eyes widen comically when he spots the Tabaxi, and he fidgets with a fishhook necklace.
- Personality:
    - Traits: Awkward but earnest, Torvyn is shy around the Tabaxi but tries to impress with tales of his catches.
    - Ideal: To prove his worth through bravery or skill.
    - Bond: His fishing boat, “The Cat’s Paw,” named after a feline legend he now associates with the Tabaxi.
    - Flaw: Overeager, often tripping over his words or nets.
- Abilities:
    - Stats: AC 10, HP 4 (1d8), Speed 30 ft. Str 11, Dex 12, Con 10, Int 9, Wis 11, Cha 13.
    - Skills: Athletics +2, Survival +2
    - Equipment: Fishing net, dagger (1d4 piercing), fishhook necklace (sentimental).
- Smitten Behavior: Torvyn stammers when addressing the Tabaxi, offering her the “finest fish” (a slightly squished mackerel) or challenging rivals to prove his devotion. He’s mesmerized by her feline grace, comparing her to a “sea cat” in his clumsy compliments.
- Campaign Tie-In: He saw a “fish-man” (sahuagin) near Blackreef Cove and might ask the Tabaxi to investigate with him, tying to the priestess plot. His boat could aid a wreck dive.
- Interaction:
    - Line: “Uh, m-miss… er, fair Tabaxi! I caught this beauty just for you. Maybe… maybe you’d sail with me someday? I’d keep the nets clear of your, uh, lovely tail!”
    - Quest Offer: Retrieve a lost net (10 gp, 1 giant crab, CR 1/8) to impress her.

**Harnic “Whistle” Tormen**

[[Poacher camp]]
- Race: Human
- Age: 33
- Gender: Male
- Occupation: Woodcutter
- Appearance: A broad-shouldered man with a scruffy beard and a whistle he uses to call his dog, Rusty. His hands are rough from axe work, but his hazel eyes soften when he sees the Tabaxi, often brushing sawdust from his shirt.
- Personality:
    - Traits: Bold but clumsy with romance, Harnic tries to woo with strength and humor.
    - Ideal: To win the Tabaxi’s heart with a grand gesture.
    - Bond: His loyal dog Rusty, a scruffy mutt who adores the Tabaxi’s scent.
    - Flaw: Overconfident, sometimes promising more than he can deliver.
- Abilities:
    - Stats: AC 11 (padded clothing), HP 5 (1d8+1), Speed 30 ft. Str 13, Dex 11, Con 12, Int 10, Wis 9, Cha 12.
    - Skills: Athletics +3, Nature +2
    - Equipment: Handaxe (1d6+3 slashing), whistle, Rusty (use Mastiff stats, CR 1/8).
- Smitten Behavior: Harnic puffs out his chest, offering to chop wood for the Tabaxi or challenging others to duels (with blunt axes). He whistles love tunes, badly, and Rusty wags his tail approvingly. He’s enchanted by her agility, calling her a “forest sprite.”
- Campaign Tie-In: He found a rune-carved log near the forest cave, linking to Lirien’s quest. He might ask the Tabaxi to explore it with him, tying to the shrine.
- Interaction:
    - Line: “Ho there, graceful one! With that tail, you’d outdance any tree I fell. Let me carve you a throne—er, stool—from the best pine! Care for a whistle duet?”
    - Quest Offer: Clear a fallen tree blocking the cave path (15 gp, 1 boar, CR 1/4) to win her favor.

    Harnic: (interrupting, puffing up) “Wood lasts longer than fish, fool! I’ll chop a forest for her—beat that, Fishhook! Rusty, show her your moves!” (Rusty barks and rolls over)  
    Both: (in unison, blushing) “Will you… uh, join me sometime?”

Campaign Integration

- Romantic Subplot: The commoners’ affection adds humor and roleplay, allowing the Tabaxi to embrace, deflect, or exploit it. Their quests tie to existing plots (sahuagin, cave shrine).
- The Auror’s Bounty: Torvyn’s boat and Harnic’s log hint at wreck and marker connections, supporting the party’s goals.
- Rune Connection/Hearthstone’s Secret: The rune log and cave path link to the ward, with Ysmeine or Lirien possibly encouraging the Tabaxi’s involvement.
- Carival and Smugglers: The commoners’ rivalry could draw Kael’s attention if the Tabaxi gains favor, adding a minor threat.
- Levity: Their clumsy courtship contrasts the campaign’s darker tones, fitting Brinehaven’s community spirit.
