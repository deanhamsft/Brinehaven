Personality and Background

- Personality Traits: Gentle and reflective, Lysa enjoys quiet company and shares local lore through proverbs (e.g., “A steady flame wards off the dark”).
- Ideal: Harmony—She believes healing and light can mend Brinehaven’s troubles.
- Bond: Her candle shop, a legacy from her half-elven mother, is her sanctuary.
- Flaw: Shyness—She avoids confrontation, relying on others to take charge.
- Background Story: Raised by her mother, an elven refugee who settled in Brinehaven, Lysa learned candle-making and herbalism. After her mother’s death, she took over The Candlekeep, noticing her candles flicker near the hearthstone—a sign she attributes to old magic. She’s unaware of the smuggling ring but senses a “dark presence” (Carival or Retch).

Role in Brinehaven

- Base: The Candlekeep, a small shop northeast of the village, surrounded by a garden of wild herbs.
- Current Focus: At 05:11 PM MDT, Lysa is lighting her shop’s candles as dusk falls, preparing to close. She’s intrigued by the party’s recent activities and may offer aid if approached.
- Reputation: Loved by villagers for her kindness, especially children like Lina Drift, who help her gather herbs.
