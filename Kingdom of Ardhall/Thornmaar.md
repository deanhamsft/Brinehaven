Thornmaar the Crimson Usurper
Red Dragonborn Tyrant-King of Ardhall  
Huge humanoid (dragonborn), lawful evil  
Armor Class 22 (natural crimson scales + obsidian plate)  
Hit Points 402 (23d12 + 230)  
Speed 40 ft., fly 80 ft. (when wings manifest), climb 40 ft.

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|30 (+10)|14 (+2)|30 (+10)|20 (+5)|18 (+4)|26 (+8)|

Saving Throws Dex +9, Con +17, Wis +11, Cha +15  
Skills Intimidation +15, Perception +11, History +12, Religion +12  
Damage Immunities fire  
Senses blindsight 60 ft., darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 21  
Languages Common, Draconic, Infernal, Abyssal; telepathy 120 ft.  
Challenge 24 (62,000 XP)  
Proficiency Bonus +7Legendary Traits

- Legendary Resistance (5/day)
- Frightful Presence (60 ft., DC 23 Wis save or frightened for 1 minute)
- Draconic Majesty. Thornmaar has advantage on Charisma checks against creatures that can see him.
- Blood of the Usurper. When Thornmaar drops a dragonborn, dragon, or metallic dragon to 0 HP, he regains 50 hit points and immediately takes a legendary action as a free action.

Actions
Multiattack. 
Thornmaar makes one bite attack, two claw attacks, and one tail attack (or Bloodreign attack).  
Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +17 to hit, reach 15 ft. Hit: 32 (4d10 + 10) piercing + 14 (4d6) fire damage.  
Claw. Melee Weapon Attack: +17 to hit, reach 10 ft. Hit: 24 (4d6 + 10) slashing damage.  
Tail. Melee Weapon Attack: +17 to hit, reach 20 ft. Hit: 19 (2d8 + 10) bludgeoning damage.  
Bloodreign (Greataxe). 
Melee Weapon Attack: +20 to hit (+3 weapon), reach 10 ft. Hit: 29 (3d12 + 13) slashing damage plus 14 (4d6) necrotic. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 23 Constitution save or its hit point maximum is reduced by the necrotic damage taken.  
Fire Breath (Recharge 5–6). Exhales fire in a 90-ft. cone. DC 25 Dex save, 91 (26d6) fire damage (half on success).

Legendary Actions (3/round)

- Detect. Make a Wisdom (Perception) check.
- Tail Attack. Make a tail attack.
- Wing Buffet (Costs 2). Each creature within 15 ft. must make a DC 25 Dex save or take 18 (2d8 + 10) bludgeoning and be knocked prone. Thornmaar can then fly up to half his speed.
- Cast a Spell (Costs 3). Cast one spell of 3rd level or lower from his innate spellcasting.

Innate Spellcasting (Cha-based, save DC 23)
At will: detect magic, speak with dead, suggestion  
3/day each: dominate person, fear, scrying  
1/day each: power word kill (only on creatures below 100 hp)Equipment

- Obsidian Plate of the Usurper (legendary armor, grants fire immunity and +3 AC)
- Bloodreign (+3 vicious greataxe, soul-drinking)
- Crown of the Crimson Sovereign (circlet of seven rubies, each holds a trapped metallic dragonborn soul; grants one free legendary action per ruby per day)

Lair Actions (while on or near the Obsidian Throne in Shal’Erel)On initiative 20 (losing ties):

- Volcanic fissures open (30-ft. line, DC 23 Dex save or 10d10 fire).
- Throne drinks blood (creatures within 60 ft. lose 3d6 HP; Thornmaar regains that amount).
- Fear of the Usurper (60-ft. radius, DC 23 Wis save or frightened).

Physical Form & Appearance

- 9 ft. tall in “humanoid” form, 15 ft. when wings and tail fully manifest.
- Scales the colour of fresh arterial blood; eyes molten gold.
- Wears the blackened bones of the platinum queen he slew as a cloak clasp.
- Voice is a furnace roar that somehow never needs to be raised; every word feels like law.

Personality & Rule

- Ideal: “Only the strongest deserve to rule. The weak exist to feed the fire.”
- Bond: The Obsidian Throne itself; he believes leaving it for more than a moon will shatter his stolen immortality.
- Flaw: Paranoid that another will do to him what he did to the previous queen.
