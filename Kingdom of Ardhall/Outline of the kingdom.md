
Kingdom of Ardhall – “The Crimson Dominion”

Once Constitutional monarchy and a chief exporter of Potash, Iron, fine marble, Obsidian, sulfur, and tree fruits from the warm southern regions, along with durables of dragonborn craft, such as specialty armors and jewelry. 

Capital  
Shal’Erel, a black-stone metropolis built inside and around the caldera of an extinct volcano. The Obsidian Throne sits in the volcanic heart-chamber, cooled by ancient sorcery yet still radiating heat.

Ruler  
Thornmaar the Crimson Usurper

Thornmaar has been amassing power since his ascent to the throne, chipping away at the rights of the nobles codified in the Pact of the great houses, a constitutional document that once limited royal powers and protected the status of the important families of the kingdom. Critical lapses in judgement have secured more power to the throne, but have had strategic implications imperiling trade and commerce in and with the kingdom, and her previous trading partners. 

Thornmaar faces fierce criticism from other nations as he has purged competent leaders from his administration in an effort to weaken opposition to his policies and eliminate any that could pose a threat to his singular rule. As a result of these purges, he has left himself with a loyal inner circle, but he has let the day to day workings of the kingdom fall into a terrible state. None in the inner circle will tell him how vulnerable the state truly is, allowing him to believe that the considerable force of his armies is the only critical metric of the Kingdom. 

Strategic observers believe that the Kingdom itself will only last another 10 to 15 years before breaking into fiefdoms, or becoming a failed state entirely. Some claim that timeline is optimistic and that the kingdom could fall much faster.


Government  
Absolute draconic monarchy enforced by the Crimson Wyrmguard (elite dragonborn paladins of conquest) and the Ash-Court (sorcerer-advisors who wield fire and blood magic).Territory

- North-eastern border: the wide, steaming Nesterin River (natural barrier and constant flashpoint with Illutuvan).
- South and west: volcanic badlands, ash deserts, and jagged peaks rich in obsidian and blood-iron.
- Climate: perpetually hot, with ash storms and rivers of liquid sulfur.

Population (~1.8 million)

- 55 % Dragonborn (mostly red, black, and gold clans)
- 20 % Kobolds (fanatically loyal servant caste)
- 15 % Tieflings and half-dragons (favored “blessed” bloodlines)
- 10 % enslaved or indentured humans, dwarves, and lizardfolk


Military

- The Ember Legions: 40,000 dragonborn heavy infantry in scale armor.
- Wyrmrider Cohorts: Dragonborn mounted on trained wyverns and young red dragons.
- Ash-Mages: Blood sorcerers.

Religion  
Thornmaar has pressed for the official worship of Tiamat, despite that worship being a minority presence in the kingdom.  
Secret cults of Bahamut are brutally suppressed.

Foreign Relations with the Imperium of Illutuvan

- State of cold war for 10 years.
- The Nesterin River is neutral on paper but both sides maintain fortified bridge-cities and river fleets.
- Raids and counter-raids are common; both empires call it “border policing.”
- Thornmaar covets Illutuvan’s northern breadbasket.

The Kingdom of Ardhall is a brutal, fire-scorched theocracy of dragonborn supremacy, ruled by a living tyrant who sits on a throne of betrayal and blood.
