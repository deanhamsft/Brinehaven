

This is an artifact created by the Dragonborn mages in the Kingdom of Ardhall

Description

                a small cube 3" per side, weighing about 300lbs. It glows a bright cherry red and gives off 38Gj (10.6 MWh) of heat energy for a full three years. It can be put into a latent state that gives off about 200 degrees of heat sustained, and can be activated to 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, or full power using a powerword once readied.

These items are very prized by the imperium as they are beginning to enter an industrial age. Thornmaar the Usurper has aggressively banned the trade of Compressed Dragonic Fire to external powers to stifle the growth and advancement of those Kingdoms.

This has created a robust and growing smuggling economy as the Imperium seeks all avenues to acquire these artifacts.
