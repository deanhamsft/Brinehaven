
Retch (Rethgar "Retch" Veyl) - The Ambitious Smuggler

Background

- Full Name: Rethgar "Retch" Veyl
- Race: Half-Elf
- Age: 38
- Gender: Male
- Occupation: Smuggler, Aspiring Warlord
- History: Retch, born to a human sailor and an elven trader, lost his left eye to a sahuagin raid, earning his nickname from the scar’s stench. Once a scout for Kael Draven’s smuggling ring, he grew tired of serving under others. His ambition shifted to establishing his own fiefdom, using the Auror’s Bounty’s treasure as capital. He learned of the wreck’s cursed pearl and its guardian from elven lore in his mother’s journals, believing the black tarry reagent—sourced from the salt marshes—can weaken the ward and bypass the ghost captain. Retch secretly funds the smuggling ring through Drenvar and Jorin, posing as the shadowy customer, while planning to betray Kael and claim the wreck himself.

Personality

- Traits: Cunning, ruthless, and charismatic when needed. Retch hides his ambition behind a scarred grin, using flattery or threats to manipulate.
- Ideal: Power through independence—Retch seeks to rule his own domain, free from Kael or Valthor’s influence.
- Bond: A hidden locket with his mother’s elven portrait, his only sentimental tie, fueling his drive.
- Flaw: Overconfidence—he underestimates the party’s persistence and the reagent’s instability.

Abilities (Level 4 Rogue)

- Stats: AC 15 (leather armor), HP 27 (4d8+4), Speed 30 ft. Str 10, Dex 16, Con 12, Int 13, Wis 11, Cha 14.
- Saving Throws: Dex +6, Int +3
- Skills: Deception +5, Stealth +6, Perception +3, Investigation +4
- Features: Sneak Attack (2d6), Cunning Action, Uncanny Dodge
- Equipment:
    - Shortsword (1d6+3 piercing)
    - Dagger (1d4+3 piercing)
    - Leather eyepatch (cosmetic)
    - 3 vials of black tarry reagent (see below)
    - Pouch with 75 gp
    - Locket (sentimental, DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) to notice elven craft)

Role in Brinehaven

- Base: Retch operates from his shack near the Stormwatch Point lighthouse (as previously detailed), now a hub for his secret plans. He visits Brinehaven sporadically, using Drenvar’s protection to avoid suspicion.
- Smuggling Ring: Retch is the true customer, paying Drenvar and Jorin 100 gp per shipment of the black tarry liquid, extracted from salt marsh algae. He plans to use it in a ritual to weaken the Auror’s Bounty’s ward, intending to seize the wreck solo.
- Current Focus: At 08:57 PM MDT, Retch is in his shack, studying the reagent vials under candlelight, unaware the party’s conflict with smugglers in the salt marshes has disrupted his supply.

The Black Tarry Reagent

- Description: A viscous, midnight-black liquid in small glass vials (1 oz each), harvested from rare algae in the salt marshes north of Brinehaven. It emits a faint, acrid odor and glows dimly in darkness.
- Properties:
    - Effect: When applied to a rune-etched object (e.g., markers, hearthstone), it temporarily disrupts magical wards (DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) to activate, lasts 1 hour, 1 vial).
    - Risk: Mishandling (DC 12 Dexterity check) causes a 5-ft radius acidic burst (2d6 acid damage, DC 13 Constitution save for half).
    - Source: Smugglers harvest it from the marshes, a 10-mile trek north, guarded by marsh hazards.
- Campaign Tie-In: The reagent weakens the elven ward, explaining sahuagin aggression. Retch believes it can disable the wreck’s guardian, but it also attracts Carival’s interest.

Campaign Integration

- The Auror’s Bounty: Retch’s goal is to claim the wreck’s treasure, using the reagent to bypass Captain Eryndor’s ghost. The party’s discovery of vials thwarts his plan.
- Rune Connection: The reagent’s effect on runes ties to the markers, suggesting Retch’s interference with the ward.
- Hearthstone’s Secret: Applying the reagent to the hearthstone could break the ward, alerting Retch to the party’s actions.
- Carival and Smugglers: Retch competes with Carival, unaware Valthor’s agent seeks the same reagent. Kael’s crew, loyal to Carival, may turn on Retch if exposed.
- Sahuagin Threat: The priestess demands the reagent, complicating Retch’s supply chain.

Interaction with the Party

- Encounter: The party encounters Retch’s smugglers in the salt marshes (10 miles north) during a random encounter (e.g., 1d4 Level 2 Bandits, CR 1/8, with 2 vials dropped). Retch himself avoids direct confrontation, fleeing if traced.
- Dialogue (If Cornered):  
    Retch:  
    (grinning through his scar, backing away)  
    "Well, well, meddlers! You’ve stumbled on my little venture, eh? This tar’s mine, and so will that wreck be. Back off, or I’ll slit more than your purses—run along now!"  
    (He uses Cunning Action to Disengage and flee if combat looms.)
- Quest Opportunity: The party can track Retch to his shack (DC 15 Wisdom (Survival)), discovering his fiefdom plans in a letter (leads to a future confrontation).

Salt Marsh Conflict

- Location: 10 miles north of Brinehaven, a soggy marsh with twisted trees and shallow pools.
- Description: The party ambushes 4 smugglers transporting reagent vials during a supply run.
- Challenge: Combat (200 XP total), DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth) to surprise, DC 12 Constitution save to avoid marsh mud (half speed).
- Loot: 2 vials of black tarry reagent, 30 gp, a smuggler map (hints at the shack).
- Consequence: Success alerts Retch, who doubles his guard (add 1 Level 3 Rogue next encounter).
