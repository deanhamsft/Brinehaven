Crimson Talon Raiders

Elite Dragonborn Commando Team of Ardhall These 5 dragonborn are Thornmaar's Talonblades—hand-picked killers from the Wyrmguard, dispatched to raid Illutuvan convoys along the Nesterin River border. Their mission: seize supplies, capture officers for interrogation, and sow terror to soften the empire for spring invasion. Led by 

Ssrath the Fangreaver, they strike fast, vanish into the badlands, and leave crimson runes (Tiamat's symbol) painted in blood.Team CR: 6 (2,300 XP total; deadly for 5 Level 4 PCs, hard for Level 5).  
Tactics: 

Ambush from high ground—Ssrath charges the lead wagon, Zethra burns the horses, Korrak smashes guards, Vexa mind-breaks captives, Drakus picks off runners. Retreat after 3 rounds or if leader falls.
