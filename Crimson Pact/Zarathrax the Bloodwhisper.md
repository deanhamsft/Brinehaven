Current State & Influence (November 2025)

- Physical Form: Still trapped in a blood-soaked obsidian shrine somewhere in the Abyss.
- Projection: Can manifest only as a disembodied voice or a tall, crimson-shadowed silhouette with burning coals for eyes.
- Cult Size: 10–12 desperate Brinehaven commoners (the Crimson Pact).
- Power Source: Every time a cultist spills sentient blood within 10 miles of Brinehaven, Zarathrax gains a fragment of strength. Ten more sacrifices and he can tear a temporary rift.

What Zarathrax Wants

1. Short-term: Ten more blood sacrifices to open a 1-hour gate so he can step through in the flesh.
2. Medium-term: Turn Brinehaven into a permanent blood-soaked fiefdom.
3. Long-term: Use the hearthstone’s dying ward as a beacon to drag the entire village into a demiplane of endless crimson tide.

How He Operates

- Whispers: Speaks directly into the minds of the cultists (and anyone wounded near the grove).
- Promises: “Spill blood and gain power. Ten hearts and I walk among you as your king.”
- Gifts: Each cultist who kills for him gains +1d6 necrotic damage on one weapon attack per day (no save, lasts until long rest).

Weaknesses

- Ward Sensitivity: The hearthstone ward (even weakened) painful to him; while it stands, he cannot cross a 10-mile radius of Brinehaven without a host body.
- Blood Dependency: If no sentient blood is spilled within 10 miles for 30 days, his whispers fade and the cult loses its boons.

Adventure Hooks

- The Tenth Heart: The cult needs one more sacrifice before November 30 moonless night. They’re eyeing the children who found the reagent crate.
- Voice in the Dark: Any party member who has bled near the grove starts hearing the whispers at night (DC 19 Wisdom save or frightened until dawn).
- Close the Gate: If the party disrupts the final ritual, Zarathrax’s silhouette appears for 3 rounds—long enough to fight a “shadow” of him (use Balor stats at 25 % HP).
