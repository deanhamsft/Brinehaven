Location  
Smack in the middle of Tinkertwist Hollow, right where the Giggle Stream bends into a perfect loop, stands a three-storey candy-cane-striped shop that looks like someone exploded a bakery inside a music box. The building itself is only twelve feet tall (perfect gnome height), but every inch is crammed with sugar-powered whimsy.Exterior

- Roof tiles are giant pastel wafer biscuits (replaced monthly).
- The chimney is a hollowed-out chocolate éclair that puffs steam smelling of vanilla and burnt sugar.
- A 6-foot peppermint windmill spins lazily on the roof, powering a tinkling music-box rendition of “Pop Goes the Weasel.”
- The front door is a slab of fudge with a liquorice-pull handle; it makes a satisfying squelch when opened.

Proprietor  
Zephyr Gumbuzzle – male rock gnome, age 87, wild white hair that smells like cotton candy, perpetually sticky fingers, and a waistcoat made of wrapped caramels.Interior  
The shop is one huge open space with three levels connected by spiral slides made of fruit-roll leather and ladders of pretzel sticks.Ground floor – “The Sugar Floor”

- Glass (rock-candy) jars line every wall, filled with:
    - Everfizz Sherbet (pinch in mouth → levitate 5 ft for 10 seconds, 3 uses)
    - Thunder-drop Jawbreakers (crunching one makes a harmless thunderclap, great for pranks)
    - Giggleroot Chews (chewer laughs uncontrollably for 1 minute)
- A copper cauldron the size of a wagon wheel constantly stirs itself, bubbling with rainbow toffee.

Middle floor – “The Chocolate Loft”

- Waterfall of warm chocolate pours from a ceiling spout into a wading pool (ankle-deep for gnomes, knee-deep for humans).
- Chocolate frogs that actually hop (non-magical, just springy marshmallow legs).
- A solid-milk-chocolate grandfather clock that chimes by dropping cocoa beans into a cup.

Top floor – “The Cloud Loft”

- Spun-sugar clouds hang from the rafters; pulling one down gives a fist-sized tuft of candy floss that replenishes every dawn.
- A hammock made of liquorice lace where Zephyr naps between batches.

Prices (all in copper pieces because “gold melts the fun”)

- Handful of any candy: 5 cp
- Chocolate frog (box of 6): 15 cp
- Everfizz Sherbet pouch (3 uses): 25 cp
- One ride down the fruit-roll slide: free (but you come out sticky)

Specials of the Week (changes every visit)

- “Boom-Berry Bombs” – pop-rocks that make a tiny fireworks display in your mouth.
- “Shrinking Mints” – taste one and shrink 1 size category for 1 minute (gnomes love watching humans suddenly fit their furniture).
