Location  
Right on the village square, directly opposite the Gumbuzzle Sweetorium and next door to the fireworks emporium (so every night ends with a free light show and the occasional soot shower).Exterior  
A three-storey, upside-down-looking building: the ground floor is narrow, the second bulges outward like a mushroom cap, and the third is a wide, round attic with a wrap-around balcony. The whole thing is painted sky-blue with brass gears the size of wagon wheels bolted to every wall. A giant, slowly turning waterwheel made of old bedframes powers a creaking mechanical sign that reads:The Cog & Cushion  
“Sleep tight, wake bright – or wake on the floor, your choice!”Proprietor  
Bimpkin Bolthole – male rock gnome, age 92, round as a pumpkin, always wearing a nightcap even at noon. He claims he’s “testing comfort levels.”Rooms & Prices (all coin gladly accepted, including foreign, chocolate, or “pretty rocks”)

1. Luxury Gnome Suite (second floor, 8 available)  
    8 sp per night  
    A round, pillow-strewn room with a circular bed that gently rocks (gnome-sized hammock included). Hot cocoa delivered by copper pipe.
2. Human-Sized Room (third-floor balcony, 4 available)  
    2 gp per night  
    Tall enough for a human, with a real mattress, a window box of edible flowers, and a bell-pull that drops warm scones from the ceiling.
3. The Flop Room (ground floor, one giant communal hall)  
    3 cp per night or “one good story”  
    A single cavernous room with:
    - A sea of mismatched cushions, bean bags, and hammocks strung between pillars.
    - A snoring chorus of travelling gnomes, a pair of sleeping ogres (visiting performers), and one very confused minotaur who still hasn’t found the exit.
    - A chalkboard where guests write the strangest dream they had that night (current top entry: “I was a teapot”).
    - One communal blanket the size of a circus tent.
    - Free earplugs carved from marshmallow (they work surprisingly well).

Food & Drink

- Breakfast: “Gearstack Pancakes” – twelve tiny pancakes layered with jam and served on a spinning cog.
- Dinner: “Cushion Stew” – whatever fell into the pot that day, guaranteed hearty and occasionally surprising.
- House Ale: “Bedtime Bounce” – one pint and you sleep like a rock; two pints and you wake up on the roof (no one knows how).

House Rules (written on a pillow in embroidery)

1. No fireworks in the Flop Room (after the 1127 incident).
2. If you snore in three-part harmony, drinks are half price.
3. Lost socks go to the Sock Gnome – no questions asked.

Special Feature  
Every dawn, Bimpkin rings a giant cushion-shaped gong. Everyone in the Flop Room is gently rolled (literally) into a central pile, given a cup of cocoa, and sent on their way with a free marshmallow earplug as a souvenir.
