Location  
On the eastern edge of the village, just far enough from the other buildings that the occasional premature explosion only rattles windows instead of removing them. The shop is a squat, dome-shaped structure built from riveted copper plates and reinforced with brightly painted iron bands. A 30-foot launch tower (made from an old windmill) sticks out of the roof like a celebratory finger pointed at the sky.Exterior

- The entire building is painted in spiralling red, gold, and emerald stripes.
- A perpetually smoking chimney shaped like a dragon’s head belches multi-coloured sparks.
- A hand-painted sign swings above the door:  
    “FIZZLEWICK’S BANG-&-WHISTLE – If it doesn’t go BOOM, it’s free!”
- Every hour on the hour a miniature rocket shoots out of the dragon chimney and explodes into the words “OPEN” in sparkling letters.

Proprietor  
Jinx Fizzlewick – female rock gnome, age 74, soot-streaked goggles permanently welded to her forehead by exploded powder residue, twin braids that end in tiny burning fuses (they never quite ignite… usually).Interior  
One cavernous room with blackened rafters and shelves that lean at alarming angles.Goods for Sale (all prices in silver because “copper gets lost in the flash”)

- Pocket Screamer (thumb-sized rocket): 5 sp – whistles, then POP, 10-ft radius harmless bang.
- Gnomeblinder Flash-Powder (small pouch): 15 sp – thrown, 15-ft bright light + blinded 1 round (DC 13 Con save).
- Skywriter Tube (3 charges): 30 sp – launches 30 ft up and spells a short word (max 8 letters) in coloured smoke.
- Thunderclap Cracker (single use): 50 sp – 20-ft radius thunder damage 2d6, DC 14 Con save for half, deafened on fail.
- Grand Festival Barrage (crate of 12 rockets): 5 gp – full fireworks show, lights up 300 ft for 1 minute, grants inspiration to everyone who watches.

House Rules (painted on a charred plank by the door)

1. No open flames inside (except Jinx’s).
2. If you break it, you buy it—and it will probably break you back.
3. Payment before launch.

Quest & Chaos Hooks

1. Jinx’s prize “Midnight Sun” rocket (biggest firework ever made) was stolen by a jealous goblin pyromancer. Retrieve it for a crate of any fireworks free.
2. The dragon-chimney has started coughing up real fire elementals (tiny, CR 1) every time the wind turns west.
3. Someone swapped all the Skywriter tubes with ones that spell rude words. Village elders are not amused.

Fizzlewick’s is loud, bright, and gloriously unsafe—the perfect gnome establishment to light up the night (literally) and give your party a spectacular distraction, bribe, or accidental village-wide incident.
