Poacher Camp: Grotfang Hollow

Overview

- Location: 10-12 miles west of Brinehaven, in the rugged hills and lower mountains, where pine forests meet rocky crags. The Silverthorn Stag, a rare creature with shimmering, thorn-like antlers tied to Aerdrie Faenya, is the target. The orc poachers, led by a cunning chieftain, have established a brutal camp to harvest the stags for their antlers, driven by a deal with a shadowy buyer (possibly Retch or Carival).
- Theme: A savage orc encampment with traps, brute force, and a moral dilemma, blending combat, stealth, and environmental stakes.
- Level Suitability: Designed for a Level 2-3 party of five (CR 1/2 to 2 encounters, totaling 800-1,000 XP, split ~160-200 XP per player).
- Time: Exploration takes 2-3 hours, fitting a dawn to mid-morning session (e.g., 6:00 AM - 9:00 AM MDT on October 22, 2025).

Camp Layout

The camp consists of six areas, connected by rocky trails and dense underbrush. Each area is 20x20 ft unless noted, with 10-15 ft-high crags or tree cover.

**Area 1: Outpost Sentry**

- Description: **A rocky ledge with a crude watchpost of lashed logs, overlooking the approach. Broken stag antlers litter the ground.**
- Inhabitants: 2 Orc (CR 1/2, Monster Manual p. 246), armed with javelins and greataxes, scanning for intruders.
- Features: A spiked pit trap (DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) to spot, DC 12 Dexterity save or 2d6 piercing from 10-ft fall).
- Challenge: Combat (100 XP total) or Stealth (DC 13) to bypass. A DC 14 Intelligence (Nature) identifies Silverthorn blood.
- Loot: 10 gp, 2 javelins.

**Area 2: Baited Gorge**

- Description: **A narrow gorge with salt licks and a trough of spoiled meat, luring stags. A tripwire spans the path.**
- Inhabitants: 1 Orc (CR 1/2) and 1 Dire Wolf (CR 1), trained to ambush prey.
- Features: Tripwire trap (DC 12 Wisdom (Perception), DC 11 Dexterity save or knocked prone, 1d6 bludgeoning from rocks).
- Challenge: Combat (150 XP) or Deception (DC 13 Charisma to mimic orc calls). The wolf’s bite (2d6+2 piercing) is a threat.
- Loot: 15 gp, a stag hoof (5 gp).

**Area 3: Skinning Pit**

- Description: **A smoky pit with a fire and racks of curing hides, reeking of blood. Orcs work with crude tools.**
- Inhabitants: 2 Orc (CR 1/2 each), skinning stags with cleavers.
- Features: A grease-slicked floor (difficult terrain, DC 12 Dexterity (Acrobatics) or fall prone).
- Challenge: Combat (100 XP total) or Intimidation (DC 14 Charisma to scare off). Cleaver attacks (1d6+3 slashing) are messy.
- Loot: 20 gp, 1 stag hide (30 gp).

**Area 4: Stag Corral**

- Description: **A rough enclosure of logs and chains, holding 2 Silverthorn Stags** (use Elk stats, CR 1/4, +1 AC from antlers).
- Inhabitants: None, but the stags charge if freed (1d6+3 bludgeoning).
- Features: A weak gate (DC 12 Strength (Athletics) to break) and a poacher’s journal (DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)) noting a “ hooded trader.”
- Challenge: Roleplay or Skill (DC 13 Wisdom (Animal Handling) to calm, DC 14 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) to unlock). Freeing them alerts the camp.
- Loot: Journal (plot item), antler shard (10 gp, heals 1d4 hp if brewed, 1 use).

**Area 5: War Tent**

- Description: **A large tent with orcish trophies (skulls, weapons) and a map table, serving as the leader’s base**.
- Inhabitants: 1 Orc War Chief (use Orc stats with Multiattack and +2 to all stats, CR 2, 45 HP), a burly leader with a notched greataxe.
- Features: A locked strongbox (DC 14 Dexterity (Thieves’ Tools)) with poacher profits and a buyer’s note (Retch’s seal).
- Challenge: Combat (450 XP) or Negotiation (DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) to bribe with 50 gp). His greataxe (2d6+4 slashing) and Aggressive trait (move 30 ft toward enemy) are dangerous.
- Loot: 50 gp, note (plot item), greataxe (nonmagical, 10 gp).

**Area 6: Signal Bluff**

- Description: **A high bluff with a bonfire and a horn, used to call reinforcements from deeper mountains.**
- Inhabitants: 1 Orc (CR 1/2), guarding the signal.
- Features: The bonfire (DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) to douse) summons 1d4+1 orcs in 4d4 rounds if lit.
- Challenge: Combat (50 XP) or Sabotage (DC 12 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) to disable). Success prevents escalation.
- Loot: 10 gp, horn (5 gp).

Dungeon Flow

- Entry (Area 1): The party faces sentries, setting a combat or stealth tone.
- Progression (Areas 2-4): Gorge, pit, and corral offer escalating fights and a moral choice.
- Climax (Areas 5-6): The war tent and bluff provide a boss fight and strategic end.
- Exit: The camp burns post-combat (DC 12 Dexterity save or 1d6 fire damage), urging a quick retreat.

Silverthorn Stag

- Description: **A majestic stag with silver, thorn-like antlers that glow faintly, revered as Aerdrie Faenya’s ward spirit.**
- Stats: Use Elk (AC 10, HP 13, Speed 50 ft, 1d6+3 bludgeoning charge), +1 AC from antlers, passive Perception 12.
- Lore: Poaching angers Aerdrie, weakening the hearthstone ward (-1 to ward effects for 1 week, DM discretion).

Campaign Integration

- Environmental Subplot: Orc poaching threatens the ward, linking to "Hearthstone’s Secret." Saving stags earns Aerdrie’s favor (+1 to Nature checks for 24 hours).
- The Auror’s Bounty: The “hooded trader” (Retch) suggests a reagent-antler trade, tying to the wreck.
- Rune Connection: Stag trails near a marker hint at elven ties, supporting the ritual.
- Carival and Smugglers: Retch’s involvement clashes with Kael’s crew, hinting at a power struggle.
- Quest Giver: Harnic “Whistle” Tormen or Drenvar (if uncorrupted) might hire the party to stop the orcs.

    Harnic: (whistling nervously, axe in hand)  
    "Oi, Tabaxi beauty! Them orc brutes are slaughterin’ Silverthorns 10 miles west—ruinin’ my hills! Help me drive ‘em off, and I’ll pay 40 gold. Maybe carve you a stag whistle too?"
