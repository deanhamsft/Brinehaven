Council Meeting Scene (Longhouse)
**The three council members sit at a weathered oak table beneath whale-oil lamps.** 
**Liora presides, Magnus counts coins, Vespera sharpens her staff.**

**the party is summoned to a council meeting, after finding and clearing a poacher camp. The Silverthorn Stag is sacred in this village, and the party has returned with several hides, which they gave to Ragna. Ragna brought the hides to the council, and despite promising the hide the true source, the council managed to divulge the information. The party enters and overhear some of the council business;**

**Liora: “The hearthstone dims. We must act.”**  
**Magnus: “Action costs coin. Who’s paying?”**  
**Vespera: “Children are whispering of black tar and scarred men. Someone here knows more than they say. ”If the party enters with proof (ledger, reagent vial, children’s map), Vespera slams the table in support, Liora demands a full hearing, and Magnus sweats—perfect for a dramatic council showdown. These three give you a balanced council: one rooted in history, one in commerce, one in justice—ready to reward, resist, or redeem based on the party’s choices.** 

**At the head of the table, Councilor Liora Thaloryn leans forward, her silver-streaked braid glinting in the lamplight. Her fingers trace the rim of a cracked hearthstone shard that serves as her gavel.
Liora (voice low, urgent):**  
**“The hearthstone dims. We must act.”**

**Magnus Holt, Harbor Master, slouches back in his chair, idly spinning a gold coin across his knuckles. His ledger lies open beside a half-empty tankard.Magnus (gruff, dismissive):**  
**“Action costs coin. Who’s paying? The village coffers are thinner than a gutted cod.”**

**Across from him, Vespera Ashborn grips her flame-topped staff so tightly the wood creaks. Her golden eyes blaze.Vespera (voice sharp as a knife):**  
**“Children are whispering of black tar and scarred men. Someone here knows more than they say.”**

**The doors creak the rest of the way open.**

**The party steps inside, boots dripping on the floorboards.** 

**Ragna Skinsmith stands in front of the council them, arms folded, a bundle of Silverthorn Stag hides slung over one shoulder like a challenge.** 

**The sacred silver fur catches the lamplight, impossible to miss.**
**Liora’s gaze snaps to the hides, as she points her finger.**  
**Magnus’s coin stops spinning.**  
**Vespera rises halfway out of her seat.**

**Ragna (gruff, but proud):**  
**“As I said; Found these in the hills. Poacher camp—orc work. These five cleared it.”** 

**She jerks a thumb at the party. “I promised ‘em the hides fair and square, but word is the Silverthorn is sacred. Figured the council oughta see ‘em first.”**

**Liora’s voice is ice.**  

**Liora: “The Silverthorn is more than sacred. It is bound to the hearthstone itself. Killing them weakens the ward.”**  

**She turns to the party. “You slew the poachers… and took trophies?”**

**Before anyone can answer, Vespera slams both palms on the table. The tankard jumps.**
**Vespera:**  
**“Enough stalling. While we argue over coin and tradition, the ward fails and children bring me this.”**  
**She produces a water-stained ledger, a broken vial of black tarry reagent, and a children’s crayon-scrawled map marked with a crude “X” 14 miles north and the words “scarred man wants cave.”**

**Magnus’s face goes the color of old parchment. A bead of sweat rolls from his temple.**

**Magnus (voice cracking):**  
**“That… that’s just a child’s scribble—”Vespera cuts him off, voice rising:**  
**“Children don’t draw sahuagin symbols by accident. And they don’t find black tar that eats through iron unless someone is smuggling it through our harbor.”Liora stands slowly, the weight of centuries in her eyes.**
**Liora:**  
**“Councilor Magnus. You assured us the docks were clean. Explain.”Magnus opens his mouth. Closes it. The coin slips from his fingers and rolls across the floor, clinking to a stop at the party’s feet.**

**Vespera turns to the party, golden eyes fierce but grateful.**  
**Vespera:**  
**“You cleared a poacher camp that was skinning sacred beasts. You brought proof of corruption that reaches these very walls. Speak. The council will hear you—all of you.”She gestures to the empty chairs opposite the councilors.**

**Liora (voice steady, but trembling with barely-contained fury):**  
**“Sit. The hearthstone dims, the ward frays, and now sacred blood stains our hands. Tell us everything. And then…”**  
**She looks to Magnus, who is suddenly very interested in his boots.**  
**“…we will decide who pays.”**

**The whale-oil lamps flicker. Outside, the wind howls like a warning.**
**The floor is yours.**
