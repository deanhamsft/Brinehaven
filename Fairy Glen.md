
Fairy Glen
Location and Description

- Distance and Direction: 6 miles south-southwest of Brinehaven, a trek through rolling hills, sparse pine forests, and marshy lowlands. The journey takes 2-3 hours on foot (DC 12 Constitution save or 1 level of exhaustion) or 1-2 hours on horseback from Woodcock Stables (40 gp rental).
- Geography: A hidden dell surrounded by ancient, gnarled trees draped in moss and glowing with bioluminescent fungi. A crystal-clear stream winds through, fed by a small waterfall that sparkles with faint iridescent light. Tiny, flower-like structures—fey homes—dot the glade, and the air hums with a subtle, playful magic. The glen is warded by a faint fey glamour, requiring a DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) check to locate the entrance (a moss-covered archway) without guidance.
- Atmosphere: Whimsical yet eerie, with laughter-like breezes and the occasional flicker of pixie lights. The rare fey presence hints at an ancient elven connection to Brinehaven’s ward, possibly tied to Aerdrie Faenya.

Inhabitants

- Fey Guardians:
    - 3 Pixies (CR 1/4, Monster Manual p. 253), mischievous but protective, led by a sprightly figure named Tinselwing. They wield Polymorph (DC 12 Wisdom save) and Invisibility, using pranks to test intruders.
    - 1 Sprite Swarm (use Swarm of Bats stats, CR 1/4, adjusted to 22 HP, 2d4 piercing with Poison Spray, DC 11 Constitution save), guarding the glen’s heart.
- Flora and Fauna: Silverthorn Stag tracks suggest occasional visits, and the stream hosts glowing fish with minor healing properties (1d4 hp if eaten raw, 1 use).

Key Features

- Fey Archway: The entrance, a mossy arch glowing with runes (DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) to identify as elven, linked to the hearthstone ward). Crossing without permission triggers a Confusion effect (DC 13 Wisdom save, 1 minute).
- Crystal Stream: Drinking from it grants a Bless effect (1 hour, 1/long rest, DC 12 Wisdom (Survival) to find the sweet spot).
- Glimmering Glade: A central clearing with pixie homes and a stone altar, where Tinselwing holds court. The altar bears a faded inscription: “The Song Awakens the Light” (hints at Ysmeine’s flute).
- Hidden Cache: A hollow tree (DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation)) contains a Potion of Healing and 20 gp, left by a forgotten traveler.

Campaign Integration

- The Auror’s Bounty: The glen’s fey magic may counter the pearl’s curse, offering a ritual site if the party allies with the pixies. Tinselwing hints at a “dark tide” (the wreck) corrupting the coast.
- Rune Connection: The archway runes match the coastal markers, suggesting a fey-elven ward network. The pixies know a chant to strengthen it (DC 15 Charisma (Performance) with aid).
- Hearthstone’s Secret: The altar’s inscription ties to the hearthstone, implying the glen’s magic can reinforce the ward against sahuagin or Retch’s reagent.
- Carival and Smugglers: Carival’s reagent smuggling may have angered the fey, who see it as a violation. The pixies might aid the party against him if appeased.
- Sahuagin Threat: The priestess avoids the glen, fearing fey magic, making it a safe haven or staging ground.

Interaction with the Party

- Encounter: At 6:00 AM MDT on October 23, 2025, the party arrives at dawn, the glen aglow with morning mist. Tinselwing and the pixies emerge, giggling, surrounding the most fearsome member (e.g., a scarred Tabaxi or hulking barbarian) with curious stares.
- Dialogue (Tinselwing):  
    Tinselwing: (tinkling laugh, hovering)  
    "Oho, big one! You stomp like a troll but shine like a star! This glen is ours—old magic, elven-made. Why trespass? Speak, or we’ll turn you into a toad!"  
    (pauses, tilting head)  
    "But… you smell of the sea’s trouble. A crate, a curse? Tell us, and we might help. Offer a gift—shiny things please us!"
- Handling Responses:
    - Persuasion (DC 13 Charisma): Offer a gift (e.g., 5 gp, a trinket). Success gains Tinselwing’s aid (chant, map to cache); failure triggers a prank (Polymorph).
    - Intimidation (DC 15 Charisma): Threaten the pixies. Success scares them into compliance; failure summons the sprite swarm.
    - Insight (DC 14 Wisdom): Read their intent. Success reveals their fear of the reagent; failure needs a gift.
- Quest Offer: Tinselwing asks the party to retrieve a stolen pixie crown from poachers in Grotfang Hollow (30 gp, ties to the orc camp), promising the chant and cache access.

Quest: Retrieve the Pixie Crown

- Objective: Recover the crown from the orc war chief’s tent (Area 5 of Grotfang Hollow).
- Challenge: Combat or Stealth (DC 14) to take it from the chief. Success yields the crown; failure alerts the camp.
- Reward: 30 gp, chant (ward boost), Potion of Healing, 200 XP.

DM Notes

- XP Total: ~150 XP (pixies 75, swarm 75), plus 200 XP for the quest, totaling 350 XP, split ~70 XP per player, suitable for Level 2-3.
- Scaling: For Level 2, reduce to 2 pixies. For Level 4, add 1 Banshee (CR 4, scaled to CR 2) as a fey guardian.
- Magic Rarity: The glen’s subtle magic (no items beyond the potion) fits the region’s scarcity, with lore as the true reward.
- Time: The 2-3 hour explore fits 6:00 AM - 9:00 AM MDT, with dawn travel.
