Group Overview

- Composition: A trio of children, aged 8-12, from diverse Brinehaven families, united by their curiosity and a shared secret.
- Names and Roles:
    1. Tobin Gale (Male Human, 12) - The leader, Mirra Gale’s nephew, bold but nervous.
    2. Lina Drift (Female Human, 10) - The quiet observer, Elda Driftwood’s granddaughter, with sharp eyes.
    3. Finn Vax (Male Half-Elf, 8) - The dreamer, Torm “Line” Vax’s son, prone to exaggeration.
- Appearance: **Ragged cloaks soaked from the storm, wide eyes, and smudged faces. The leader carries a stick “sword,” the girl a seashell, and the other a carved wooden fish.**
- Personality: A mix of awe, fear, and excitement. They’re drawn to the party’s most fearsome member, seeing them as a hero from their tales, and are eager yet hesitant to share their discovery.
- Motivation: They stumbled upon a clue while playing near the docks, believing it ties to the “ghost ship” rumors, and want to help but fear adult disbelief.

Important Information

- Discovery: **While exploring the rocky shore near the docks during the storm, the children found a waterlogged crate washed ashore, marked with a sahuagin symbol and containing a broken vial of black tarry reagent. Inside was a scrap of parchment with a crude map showing a cave 14 miles north (Lirien’s shrine) and a note: “Deliver to the scarred one”** (Retch). This suggests Retch’s smuggling ties to the sahuagin and his interest in the shrine’s ward.
- Significance: The reagent and map link to Retch’s plan to claim the Auror’s Bounty, the sahuagin priestess’s alliance, and the weakening ward. The party can use this to expose the smuggling ring (Drenvar, Jorin) or prepare for the shrine quest.

Interaction with the Party

- Encounter: The Brinehaven Hearth is quiet, the hearthstone’s glow a warm contrast to the storm’s roar. The children slip inside, shivering, and gravitate toward the most fearsome party member (e.g., a Tabaxi with a scarred muzzle or a hulking half-orc). **A child steps forward, clutching his stick, while two others peek from behind.**
- Dialogue:  
    the leader: (swallowing hard, voice shaky)  
    **"You—the big one with the claws! We… we saw somethin’ by the docks. A crate, all slimy, with fish-man marks. Me and Lina and Finn found it tonight, and there’s a map inside! We think it’s about that ghost ship!"** 
    the girl: (whispering, holding up the seashell)  
    **"It stank like tar, and the vial broke. But the map… it points north, way up past the hills. We didn’t tell the grown-ups—they’d just yell."**
    the other boy: (eyes wide, waving the fish): **"It’s a treasure map! The scarred man’s gonna steal it, and the fish-men are helping! You gotta stop him! —you’re like the hero in my stories!"**
    the leader: (pleading):
    **"Please, don’t let ‘em get us in trouble. Can you take it? We’ll show you where it is if you promise to be careful!"**
- Handling Responses:
    - If the Party Agrees (Persuasion DC 12 or Kindness):  
        Tobin: (grinning)  
        "Thanks, hero! It’s by the big rock near the docks—hidden under seaweed. We’ll wait here tomorrow night to hear what you find. Don’t tell Sheriff Holt, okay?"
    - If the Party Hesitates (Neutral or Failed Persuasion):  
        Lina: (frowning)  
        "Please… it’s scary out there. The fish-men might come back. We’ll give you my shell if you go—my grandma says it’s lucky!"
    - If the Party Refuses (Intimidation or Dismissal):  
        Finn: (tearing up)  
        "But… you’re supposed to be the strong one! Fine, we’ll hide it again…"  
        (They retreat, leaving the crate hint, open to later persuasion.)

Roleplay Enhancements

- Quirks: Tobin postures like a knight, Lina clutches her shell nervously, and Finn embellishes with wild gestures. They giggle when the fearsome member roars or flexes.

- Ties to Campaign:
    - The reagent ties to Retch’s smuggling and the sea hag’s lair.
    - The map aligns with Lirien’s shrine quest, reinforcing "Hearthstone’s Secret."
    - The sahuagin link suggests the priestess’s involvement, tying to "Rune Connection."
    - Drenvar’s corruption is hinted at by their distrust.

Quest: Retrieve the Crate

- Objective: Investigate the docks, retrieve the crate, and decipher its contents.
- Location: Near the docks, under a large rock by the tide line, 0.5 miles from the Hearth.
- Challenge:
    - Stealth: DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth) to avoid 1 sahuagin scout (CR 1/2) patrolling at night.
    - Perception: DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) to find the crate, DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) to decode the map.
    - Combat: If spotted, the scout summons 1d4 more in 1d4 rounds.
- Reward: Crate contents (map, vial fragment), 20 cp from the children (pooled savings), 150 XP.

DM Notes

- Level Range: The quest suits Level 2-3 (CR 1/2, 100-200 XP). Scale with 1 Thug (CR 1/2) for Level 4.
- Pacing: The encounter fits, with the quest taking 1-2 hours.
- Fearsome Appeal: The children’s awe (e.g., “You’re scarier than the fish-men!”) boosts the chosen member’s confidence, granting inspiration if roleplayed.
- Humor: Their wide-eyed admiration and Finn’s tall tales add levity, balancing the storm’s tension.
