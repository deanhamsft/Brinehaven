**The Illutuvan Imperium**

*“Through Vigilance, Unity; Through Unity, Victory”*

**Overview**

The Illutuvan Imperium is a young, aggressive, highly militarized human-led empire that has spent the last 240 years hammering disparate kingdoms, city-states, and tribal lands into a single iron-hard realm. It is a nation that lives with its hand on its sword hilt. Every citizen owes three years of mandatory legionary service between ages 18–25, and the standing professional legions are among the best-drilled forces on the continent.

Government: 

Enlightened stratocracy. The Emperor (currently Aurelian val Illutuvan IV “the Winter Eagle”) rules as First General of the Imperium with the advice of the Consilium Bellum composed of the nine Marshal-Commanders of the Grand Legions and the heads of the great noble houses. The Senate of the Hundred Cities exists, but its powers are limited to taxation and civil law; war and foreign policy remain the Emperor’s alone. Heavily influenced by the emperor, the Senate rarely stands against him in matters of taxation, with good reason as the emperor is well versed in the shades and shadows of economies. 

**Capital:**
Illutuvan, a colossal fortified city built on seven hills, beside the Hadtou Ocean and near the mount of the Auren river.

Official languages: 
Common (Illutuvan dialect), Elvish (Western Sylvan), Infernal (legal and mercantile use among tiefling houses).

Currency: 
The golden Solari (a sunburst coin bearing Aurelian’s profile), silver Legions, and copper March-marks.

**Peoples of the Imperium**

- Humans (68 %): The backbone of the legions and the bureaucracy.
- Wood Elves (15 %): Integrated centuries ago after the War of Hollow Leaves. Many serve as skirmishers, scouts, and druidic battle-mages. The great forest of Vaelanthar still belongs to the elven Crown-Princes, but they swear fealty to the Emperor.
- Tieflings (12 %): Descendants of ancient Mephistophelean contracts. They dominate the Imperium’s banking, arcane academia, and intelligence services. The ruby-skinned House Vacharn alone finances half the legions.
- Gnomes (3 %): Concentrated in the university-city of Caer Gylynn and the gnomish artillery corps (the famed “Thunder Companies”).
- Mountain Dwarves (2 %): Clans in the Cenalus foothills; master siege engineers and smiths who supply the legions with dwarven steel and black-powder weapons (rare but devastating).

Geography & Borders

- North & Northwest: The Orcish Horde-lands of Kragnor-Zul. Endless raids and one full-scale invasion (the Five-Year Blood Winter, 1178–1183 IR) keep the border legions sharp.
- West: The towering Cenalus Mountains, crossed only by three fortified passes. Beyond the western slopes runs the Nesterin River, the legal boundary.
- Southwest: The fractured Dragonborn kingdom of Ardhall, now the Domain of Thornmaar the Crimson Usurper. Thornmaar said to have made a deal with a devil and now sits the Obsidian Throne. Dragonborn mercenaries and exiles trickle into Illutuvan service, nursing grudges. ![[Ardhall Current.jpg]]
- South: The warm, pirate-infested Bon Gulf.
- East: The stormy Hadtou Ocean. The Imperial Navy (primarily tiefling-crewed black galleons) keeps the sea lanes open.

Emperor Aurelian IV val Illutuvan, “the Winter Eagle”

Age 39. 
A military genius who has never lost a major battle. Rose from among the royal family to Emperor in 19 years. Said to have the blood of ancient silver dragons (explaining his pale eyes and resistance to cold). Rules from the Adamantine Palace atop the Seventh Hill. Wears plain legionary scarlet in public; only the thin circlet of imperial office marks his rank. Speaks rarely, but when he does entire legions fall silent.

The Nine Legiones Grandis (active)

1. I Legionem “Aurelian’s Own” – heavy infantry, the Emperor’s household guard
2. II Legionem “Ironwood” – wood-elf archers and druidic shock troops
3. III Legionem “Hellforge” – tiefling battle-mages and infernal artillery
4. IV Legionem “Thunderers” – gnomish cannon and bombards  
    5–9. V–IX Legiones – mixed provincial heavy legions stationed along the orc frontier

**Religion**
The Imperium officially reveres the Triadic Compact:

- Erathis (goddess of civilization and law) – primary human deity
- Sehanine (moon and autumn, patron of wood elves)
- Asmodeus (lord of contracts and rightful dominion) – openly worshipped by tiefling nobility, tolerated elsewhere

> [!NOTE]
> The worship of chaotic or destructive deities is punishable by enslavement in the frontier legions.

**Notable Locations**

- Illutuvan (capital)
- Vaelanthar – ancient wood-elf forest, source of living siege-towers and treant auxiliaries
- Caer Gylynn – gnomish university-city of floating towers and perpetual explosions
- Port Vacharn – tiefling free-city on the Bon Gulf, richer than the capital
- The Crimson Pass – heavily fortified gap where three legions stand permanent watch against Ardhall
- Hadtou Shipyards – birthplace of the imperial dreadnoughts

Current Tensions (great campaign hooks)

1. Emperor Aurelian has no legitimate heir; the noble houses circle like wolves.
2. Thornmaar of Ardhall is secretly negotiating with Grushnak orc warlords for an anti-Illutuvan alliance.
3. A new orc Warchief, Gorvex One-Eye, is uniting the northern tribes faster than ever before.
4. Whispers that the Emperor’s silver-dragon blood is thinning and he seeks a dragonborn bride—or conquest—to renew it.
5. Tiefling banking houses are pushing for open war with Ardhall to seize its mythral mines; wood elves want peace at almost any cost.

The Illutuvan Imperium stands at the glittering, dangerous height of its power, an empire forged in fire and discipline.
