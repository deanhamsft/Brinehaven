
**Magistrate-Intendant Seraphine Valtari**

**Human Rogue (Mastermind) 9**
Trade & Legal Advisor  
Medium humanoid (human), lawful neutral  
Armor Class 14 (stylish court armour)  
Hit Points 52 (8d8 + 16)  
Speed 30 ft.

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|10 (+0)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|17 (+3)|

Saving Throws Int +7, Wis +5  
Skills Deception +6, History +7, Insight +5, Investigation +7, Persuasion +9* (Expertise)  
Senses passive Perception 12  
Languages Common, Elvish, Dwarvish, Illutuvani Trade Tongue  
Challenge 5 (1,800 XP)Innate Spellcasting (Charisma).  

At will: 
detect thoughts (only surface thoughts, no save),
prestidigitation  
3/day: zone of truth (DC 15)  
1/day: geas (only for trade contracts)

Legal Authority. Once per long rest, Seraphine can declare a contract, charter, or tariff binding under imperial law. A creature that willingly signs must make a DC 17 Wisdom save or be magically compelled to uphold the agreement (as geas without damage).

Actions  
Multiattack.

Seraphine makes two rapier attacks.  
Masterwork Rapier. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage.  
Binding Writ (Recharge 5–6). Seraphine presents an official document to one creature within 30 ft. The target must succeed on a DC 17 Charisma save or be stunned until the end of its next turn as it is overwhelmed by bureaucratic fine print.

Equipment

- Courtier’s rapier
- Stylish half-plate under silk robes
- Imperial seal ring
- Ledger of proposed tariffs (already lists every shop in Brinehaven)
- 200 gp in Illutuvan gold crowns

Personality  
Cool, impeccably dressed, speaks in measured clauses. Always has a quill ready and a smile that never reaches her eyes. Sees people as numbers on a balance sheet.

Harbor & Naval Advisor  
Medium humanoid (human), lawful neutral  
Armor Class 17 (breastplate + cloak)  
Hit Points 78 (12d10 + 24)  
Speed 30 ft., swim 30 ft.

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|17 (+3)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|16 (+3)|

Saving Throws Str +6, Con +5  
Skills Athletics +6, Intimidation +6, Perception +6, Survival +6  
Senses passive Perception 16  
Languages Common, Aquan, Illutuvani Trade Tongue  
Challenge 6 (2,300 XP)Sea Legs. Darius has advantage on ability checks and saving throws made to resist being knocked prone or moved against his will while on a ship or in water.  
Admiral’s Command. As a bonus action, Darius can grant one ally within 60 ft. advantage on its next attack roll or ability check before the end of its next turn.

Actions  
Multiattack. 

Darius makes three attacks: two with his cutlass and one with his off-hand dagger.  
+1 Cutlass. Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (1d6 + 5) slashing damage.  
Dagger (off-hand). Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage.  
Boarding Pike. Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 10 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (1d10 + 4) piercing damage.  
Command the Tide (Recharge 5–6). Darius roars an order. Up to six friendly creatures within 60 ft. who can hear him can immediately use their reaction to move up to their speed or make one weapon attack.

Equipment

- +1 cutlass (naval officer’s sabre)
- Breastplate with salt-stained admiral’s coat
- Boarding pike (functions as lance when charging)
- Spyglass, signal whistle, naval charts of Brinehaven harbor (already marked for two new imperial warship berths)
- 150 gp + bottle of 50-year-old Illutuvan rum

Personality  
Weather-beaten, loud, smells of tar and rum. Speaks in clipped naval jargon (“Belay that nonsense!”). Treats land as a temporary inconvenience between decks.
