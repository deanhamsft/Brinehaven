Appearance & Personality

- Age 148, stout as a barrel (4'8", 220 lbs), braided black beard streaked with silver and iron filings, eyes like polished obsidian.
- Wears: Dwarven plate etched with clan runes, oil-stained apron overtop, perpetual grease smudge on forehead.
- Traits: Gruff, pragmatic, obsessively detail-oriented. Speaks in clipped sentences, gestures with hammer.
- Ideal: “Stone remembers. Empire endures.”
- Bond: Last survivor of Clan Ironvein; the mine is his family’s legacy.
- Flaw: Unyielding—will not compromise on “dwarven rights” to the mine or belt.

Current Motives (December 1, 2025)

- Primary Goal: Reclaim and re-open Khazad-Durak under imperial charter.
- Immediate Issue: Scouts report the mine is already cleared (party’s work). He demands the elven-runed dwarven belt as “clan property returned to its forge.”
- Plan:
    1. Survey the mine (Dec 2–4) with 10 sappers.
    2. Petition Captain-Legate Cassian Duval for a formal reclamation writ.
    3. If the party refuses the belt, “seize it as imperial asset” with marine backup.
- Endgame: Restart mithril production, making Brinehaven the empire’s northern forge-hub.

Role in Brinehaven

- Base: Temporary camp near Woodcock Stables; moves to mine once secured.
- Public Face: Lectures villagers on “dwarven engineering superiority” while his sappers repair docks for free (PR stunt).
- Interactions:
    - With Party: “Ye cleared my clan’s tomb? Good. Hand over the Girdle of Deep Accord—it belongs in the heart-forge.”
    - With Council: Pressures Liora for “historical restitution”; offers Vespera mine-safety jobs for orphans.
    - With Expedition: Loyal to Cassian but prioritizes dwarven claims.

Combat Tactics

- Dons power armor immediately (bonus action).
- Thunder gauntlets for ranged suppression while sappers flank.
- Heat metal on enemy weapons; fabricate rubble into barriers.
- Grimspark scouts/delivers caltrops.
