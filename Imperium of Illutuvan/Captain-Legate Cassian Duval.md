Proficiency Bonus +3
Traits
Imperial Bearing. 

Cassian has advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) and Charisma (Persuasion) checks when interacting with commoners or those unfamiliar with imperial protocol.  

Tactical Assessment. As a bonus action, Cassian can study one creature within 60 ft. Until the start of his next turn, he and his allies have advantage on attack rolls against that creature. 

Second Wind (1/short rest). Cassian regains 1d10 + 6 hit points.  
Action Surge (1/short rest). Cassian takes one additional action on his turn.
Actions
Multiattack. Cassian makes two longsword attacks.  
Longsword. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage, or 8 (1d10 + 3) if used two-handed.  
Heavy Crossbow. Ranged Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, range 100/400 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d10 + 1) piercing damage.  
Commander's Strike (3/short rest). When Cassian takes the Attack action, he can forgo one attack to direct an ally within 30 ft. to use their reaction to make a weapon attack against a target Cassian can see.ReactionsParry. When Cassian is hit by a melee attack, he can use his reaction to add 4 (+ proficiency bonus) to his AC against that attack, potentially causing it to miss.

Equipment

- Imperial Half-Plate (engraved with the twin-headed eagle)
- +1 Longsword (“Eagle’s Talon,” family heirloom)
- Heavy Crossbow with 20 bolts
- Sealed Imperial Writs (legal authority to commandeer resources, levy taxes, and execute “threats to the Realm”)
- Signet Ring (official stamp for charters and orders)
- Pouch with 100 gp (Illutuvan gold crowns)

Personality & Tactics

- Traits: Urbane, ruthlessly efficient, always three steps ahead. Speaks in measured tones, as if explaining simple truths to children.
- Ideal: “Order is the foundation of prosperity. Chaos serves no one.”
- Bond: Loyal to Marshal-Commander Aeloria Vex’thalan, whom he sees as the future emperor.
- Flaw: Arrogant—underestimates “frontier rabble” like the party.

In Combat: Opens with Commander's Strike to buff marines, uses Action Surge for burst damage, and Second Wind to rally. Prefers to command from behind his Ironsides century. Social: Uses Persuasion to sway villagers (“The Realm brings roads and safety”), Intimidation for resisters (“Sign the charter, or the marines will”). Always has a quill ready to draft binding agreements.Cassian Duval is the Empire’s velvet glove over an iron fist—polished, professional, and utterly unstoppable unless the party exploits his overconfidence or sows dissent among his troops. Perfect for a tense political showdown.
