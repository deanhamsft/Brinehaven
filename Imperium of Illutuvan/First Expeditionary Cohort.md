Commanding Officer (on site)  
Captain-Legate Cassian Duval – human male, 34, acting with Marshal-Commander Ael Erivox Vex’thalan’s full authority.

Forces Present (330 personnel)

- 1st Century “Ironsides” – 100 heavy legionaries
- 2nd Century “Tidebreakers” – 100 marines
- Dwarven mining detachment – 30 veterans led by Thargrim Ironvein
- Two imperial advisors: Magistrate-Intendant Seraphine Valtari (trade) and Commodore-Admiral (ret.) Darius Korr (harbour fortification)

What the Expedition Currently Believes (as of Nov 10)

- The dwarven mine is still abandoned and dangerous.
- Local smugglers are “disorganized rabble” easily swept aside.
- Brinehaven is ripe for annexation: no sheriff, one council seat empty, sahuagin raids increasing.

What the Party Knows (and the Empire Does Not)

- The mine is cleared.
- Kael and his entire ring are dead.
- The party holds the elven-runed belt, the missing key to the ward.
- The village is therefore one ritual away from being magically protected against both sahuagin and any demonic incursion.

Immediate Consequences

1. The Dwarven Detachment’s Shock  
    When Thargrim Ironvein’s scouts reach Khazad-Durak in two days, they will find:
    - Freshly killed monster corpses
    - The mine’s central vault already looted
    - The party’s tracks (unless expertly hidden)  
        This will be reported back to Cassian Duval as “unknown adventurers have already claimed imperial property.”
2. The Belt  
    The dwarves recognize the runework instantly:  
    “That’s the Girdle of the Deep Accord, forged in friendship between Clan Ironvein and the Sea-Elves of old. It belongs in the mine’s heart-forge to rekindle the great ward.”  
    They will demand it be surrendered to the Empire as “rightful reclamation.”
3. The Power Vacuum  
    With Kael dead and no obvious criminal faction left, Erival Var Handl is now the only serious candidate for sheriff.  
    The council vote on November 15 is almost certain to install him, unless the party intervenes.
4. Imperial Timeline
    - Dwarves discover the cleared mine.
    - Cassian Duval holds his public audience and learns the party holds the belt.
    - The Empire issues a formal writ claiming the belt and all contents of the mine as “imperial assets.”
    - Sheriff vote + emergency council session to decide the belt’s fate.

The Choice Facing the Party
Captain-Legate Cassian Duval will send a polite but iron-clad invitation to the party:

“The Realm of Illutuvan requests the presence of the individuals who liberated Khazad-Durak and recovered the Girdle of the Deep Accord.  
Present yourselves and the artefact at the Longhouse, or be declared enemies of the Crown.”
