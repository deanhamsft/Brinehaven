Once I was a simple man, following just my moral compass as a common doctor then the reign of Thornmaar began. It became harder for the followers of the old guard to survive targeted famine, arrests, even so far as “disappearances” if you were a boisterous dissident. 
After public outcry, something had to be publicly and legally done, so I knew law was stated and any, and all decisions found in violation of this new law were “granted“ “Thornmaar’s Mark“ a brand applied to the center of the forehead bearers of the mark have come together to form a monastic order.
“The Order of The Ashen Seal”
The name is meant to apply to the burning of the new and the protection of the old there are “the branded“ who are the initiates. The full monks are called “Cinder hands“ the agents are dubbed “wardens“ and our founders are called “Ashbearers“
I am Ashbearer-Warden Kairon Tyvess. 
And my life belongs to Ardhall.

bearers of the mark are considered outlaws in Ardhall, and thus outside the protection of the law while in that kingdom, which is why many of my order have come north to the Imperium where they have a certain distinction among the lowborn who see them as champions of the old kingdom.

The Order of the Ashen Seal run harassing raids south through little known paths and sea routes. Disrupting and sabotaging Thornmaar's forces south of the Nesterin river, other members run smuggling routes to move Compressed Dragonic Fire (a much desired energy source) into Illutuvan to force Ardhall to either face the Imperium in open war or overthrow the usurper, and also as a funding source for their raids.
