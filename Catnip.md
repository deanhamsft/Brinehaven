Example Homebrew Item: Pouch of Catnip

- Wondrous Item, Common (50 gp): Contains 5 doses. A Tabaxi (or feline creature) inhaling a pinch must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom save or spend 1 minute in euphoric distraction (as above Stimulation phase). Allies can use it to cast Calm Emotions (targeting cat-like creatures only) by inverting the pouch. Inspired by pipeweed variants.
