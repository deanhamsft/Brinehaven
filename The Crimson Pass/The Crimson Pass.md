
A high broad verdant valley between the high peaks of the southern end of the Cenalus Mountains
overlooked by the abandoned trade city of Grethmaar, a once prosperous center for routes between Ardhall and Illutuvan.

**Speech of Thornmaar**

"Men and allies of Illutuvan! Bow now, and tremble before the might of Thornmaar of Ardhall and the Obsidian throne! I care not to grovel before you in some name of trade or commerce! I would rather you pay me tribute as subscription for peace, or face the wrath of my Dragonborn warriors and the horror of my Tiefling sorcerers! Your hearts will fail before our onslaught, your mighty men are but children before us. Your magicians pale before our august masters! Take heed, and be wise, pay our just demands and avoid slaughter for the sake of your miniscule pride and leave not your children without fathers in your cities, lest we come and slave them to pay the debt of the minor effort to come and kill you!"


**Speech of Aurelian IV val Illutuvan**

"Mighty Dragonborn and allies to Ardhall! listen and sue for peace, resist this usurper! Do not follow in his folly! Surely Illutuvan shall pay a price for victory, but do not give credence to the deception of the criminal on the throne. The battle will cost dearly! Many men and Dragonborn, Tiefling and Dwarf shall spend the last of their blood on this field before end of day. Each of you think, do not imagine that you shall be unharmed, for the sword cuts deep against all flesh! I say again, rebel against this pretender on your throne! A Dragonborn of malice and chaos! Beholden to dark forces in exchange for power over you! Take it back!

Brighter days may lay ahead, recalling the former fellowship enjoyed between our peoples. 

But behold! comes now the true son of Ardhall, orphaned by malice, refined in battle, built up by trials and troubles, the true heir and rightful inheritor of the throne! Vulkaan Ardhall!"
