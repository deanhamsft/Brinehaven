
It's a CR 5 (1,800 XP) medium humanoid (any race) of any alignment, perfect for DMs as a quirky alchemist villain, eccentric mentor, or rival tinkerer in a gnome city or arcane academy. These wizards obsess over changing matter, often carrying a signature Transmuter's Stone for buffs.

- Armor Class: 12 (15 with mage armor)
- Hit Points: 40 (9d8)
- Speed: 30 ft.
- Saving Throws: Int +6, Wis +4
- Skills: Arcana +6, History +6
- Senses: Passive Perception 11
- Languages: Any four languages

Traits

- Spellcasting: The transmuter is a 9th-level spellcaster (Int-based; spell save DC 14, +6 to hit). Prepared spells (transmutation spells marked with *):  
    Cantrips (at will): light, mending, prestidigitation, ray of frost  
    1st level (4 slots): chromatic orb, expeditious retreat*, mage armor  
    2nd level (3 slots): alter self*, hold person, knock**  
    3rd level (3 slots): blink*, fireball, slow**  
    4th level (3 slots): polymorph*, stoneskin  
    5th level (1 slot): telekinesis**
 
    
- Transmuter's Stone: Carries a crafted magic stone granting one of:
    - Darkvision (60 ft.)
    - +10 ft. speed (while unencumbered)
    - Proficiency in Con saves
    - Resistance to acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder (choice changes with benefit)  
        Casting a 1st-level+ transmutation spell lets it swap the stone's effect (if held).
        
        

Actions

- Quarterstaff (Melee): +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 2 (1d6-1) bludgeoning (or 3/1d8-1 two-handed).
    

This NPC shines in combat by self-buffing with the stone (e.g., resistance or speed), then blasting with fireball or polymorph, or controlling with slow/hold person. Out of combat, it could brew potions, transmute gold (via fabricate if prepared), or experiment chaotically—ideal for your gnome city shops! Note: The 2022 Monsters of the Multiverse reprints it with minor tweaks (e.g., 49 HP on 11d8). For player-facing transmutation, see the School of Transmutation wizard subclass in the Player's Handbook (which inspired this stone).
