Carival Stanislain's Lair:
The Shack of Torments

Carival Stanislain, a chaotic evil level 5 human sorcerer, has crafted a deceptive and sinister lair that begins with an innocuous surface structure hiding a subterranean torture dungeon. The lair is designed for secrecy, experimentation, and devotion to his patron deity, Erythnul (the chaotic evil god of envy, malice, slaughter, and panic). The total layout includes 7 rooms: 1 aboveground shack serving as the entrance, and 6 interconnected underground chambers. The underground section is accessed via a hidden staircase in the shack, warded with illusory magic (e.g., silent image to disguise the entrance as a simple floorboard) and trapped with a glyph of warding (explosive runes, DC 13 to detect/disarm, dealing 3d8 fire damage on failure).The underground dungeon is carved from damp stone, lit sporadically by ever-burning torches enchanted with continual flame. The air is thick with the scent of blood, incense, and arcane residues. Walls are etched with chaotic runes that enhance Carival's spellcasting (+1 to spell attack rolls while in the lair). Echoes of distant screams (from illusory minor illusion effects) deter intruders. The rooms are connected via narrow, twisting corridors (5 ft. wide), some barred with iron doors (AC 19, 27 HP, locked with DC 14 locks that Carival can open magically).For gameplay purposes, assume standard D&D 5e mechanics. Carival might patrol the lair or be found in the study (50% chance), chapel (30%), or torture room (20%). He uses spells like fireball, suggestion, and hold person to defend his domain, often summoning fiendish allies via summon lesser demons.

Room Layout Overview

I've structured the rooms in a logical flow, starting from the entrance. The underground forms a rough loop: Stairway leads to Room 2 (Entry Hall), which branches to Rooms 3–7, with Room 7 looping back to Room 2 for easy navigation.

This layout allows for tactical gameplay: Narrow corridors favor Carival's area spells, while rooms provide ambush points. If expanding, add minions like 2–3 cultists (commoners with fanatic loyalty) or traps like pit falls leading to spiked pits (2d6 piercing + 2d6 falling). Adjust difficulty for your party's level—Carival himself is a CR 4 threat (use the mage stat block, swap spells for sorcerer list: ray of frost, shield, misty step, scorching ray, fireball).
