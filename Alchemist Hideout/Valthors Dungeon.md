Valthor Krenn's Lair:
The Shack of Torments
[[Valthor Krenn]]
Valthor Krenn, has crafted a deceptive and sinister lair that begins with an innocuous surface structure hiding a subterranean torture dungeon. The lair is designed for secrecy, experimentation, and devotion to his patron deity, Erythnul (the chaotic evil god of envy, malice, slaughter, and panic). The total layout includes 7 rooms: 1 aboveground shack serving as the entrance, and 6 interconnected underground chambers. The underground section is accessed via a hidden staircase in the shack, warded with illusory magic (e.g., silent image to disguise the entrance as a simple floorboard) and trapped with a glyph of warding (explosive runes, DC 13 to detect/disarm, dealing 3d8 fire damage on failure).The underground dungeon is carved from damp stone, lit sporadically by ever-burning torches enchanted with continual flame. The air is thick with the scent of blood, incense, and arcane residues. Walls are etched with chaotic runes that enhance Valthor's spellcasting (+1 to spell attack rolls while in the lair). Echoes of distant screams (from illusory minor illusion effects) deter intruders.
