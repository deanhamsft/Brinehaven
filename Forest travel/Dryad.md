NPC Character Sheet: Elowen the Whispering Oak

Name: Elowen  
Race: Dryad (Fey)  
Alignment: Neutral Good  
Role: Guardian of the Ancient Grove  
Challenge Rating Equivalent: 1 (inspired by standard fantasy RPG stats, e.g., D&D 5e Dryad) Appearance

Elowen appears as a graceful, ethereal woman with skin like smooth bark in shades of soft brown and green. Vines and blooming flowers weave through her long, leafy hair, which shifts colors with the seasons—vibrant green in spring, golden in autumn. Her eyes glow with a gentle emerald light, and delicate leaves adorn her form like natural clothing.

Background
Elowen is the spirit guardian of an ancient oak at the center of a secluded forest grove. She has watched over the woods for centuries, protecting it from loggers, blights, and unnatural threats. Shy and reclusive, she prefers to observe intruders from afar, using her charms to redirect harmless travelers or enlist aid against dangers. She speaks in a soft, rustling voice like wind through leaves and harbors a deep love for all natural life.Bonded Tree: A massive, ancient oak. Elowen is mystically tied to it—if the tree is harmed, she suffers the same damage. Destroying it would drive her to madness or death.

Personality

- Traits: Gentle and nurturing toward nature; curious about kind-hearted visitors; fiercely protective when her grove is threatened.
- Ideal: "The forest's balance must be preserved at all costs."
- Bond: Her oak tree and the creatures of the grove.
- Flaw: Distrustful of outsiders, especially those carrying axes or fire; can be manipulative with charms to avoid direct conflict.

Abilities & Traits

- Innate Magic: Can cast nature-themed spells (e.g., at will: minor illusions of plants/flowers; several times per day: entangle vines, create healing berries; once per day: harden bark for protection, vanish into undergrowth, enchant a club with magic).
- Speak with Beasts and Plants: Communicates freely with animals and flora.
- Tree Stride: Steps magically into one tree and emerges from another within 60 feet.
- Fey Charm: Can enchant a humanoid or beast to view her as a friend and protector (they resist with willpower).
- Magic Resistance: Advantage against spells and magical effects.
- Vulnerabilities: Weak to fire; tied to her tree (cannot stray far without weakening).

Combat Style

Prefers avoidance and enchantment over violence. She entangles foes in roots, charms them to leave or fight on her behalf, or strides through trees to ambush. In melee, she wields a simple wooden club (magically enhanced for greater impact).Hooks for Adventure

- Players enter her grove seeking rare herbs—she might charm one to help fend off a blight.
- Loggers threaten her tree; she enlists adventurers as allies.
- She offers guidance or rewards (magical acorns, healing berries) to those who prove respectful to nature.

This sheet is adaptable for tabletop RPGs like D&D or Pathfinder—use as a peaceful ally, quest giver, or enigmatic encounter!
