Overview

- Location: 4 miles northeast of Brinehaven, in a dense, shadowy grove surrounded by twisted oaks and a circle of rune-carved stones. The air hums with an unsettling energy, and a faint red glow pulses from a central altar. The journey takes 1.5-2 hours on foot (DC 12 Constitution save or 1 level of exhaustion) or 1 hour on horseback from Woodcock Stables (40 gp rental).
- Purpose: Formed by disillusioned Brinehaven commoners seeking power to escape their hardscrabble lives, the Crimson Pact worships a minor demon lord, Zarathrax the Bloodwhisper, promising strength and dominion. They aim to siphon energy from the hearthstone ward, believing it will amplify their rituals and grant them influence over the village—or beyond.
- Theme: A chaotic blend of desperation and ambition, with crude rituals and a growing threat to Brinehaven’s stability.
- Level Suitability: Designed for a Level 3-4 party (CR 1/8 to 1 encounters, totaling 800-1,000 XP for 4-6 players, adjusted for 5 at ~160-200 XP each).

Cult Membership

The cult comprises 10 members, mostly commoners from Brinehaven, with a mix of backgrounds and motivations. They are led by a more experienced figure.

1. Korvax “Bloodhand” Trell (Male Human, 35, Level 2 Cult Fanatic)
    - Role: Leader, a former dockworker turned zealot after a sahuagin attack left him scarred.
    - Motivation: Seeks vengeance and power to protect Brinehaven his way.
    - Stats: AC 13 (padded), HP 22 (4d8), Speed 30 ft. Skills: Religion +2, Intimidation +3. Spells: Inflict Wounds, Shield of Faith. Equipment: Dagger, cult robes.

2-5. Grim Followers (4 Male/Female Humans, 25-40, Level 1 Cultists)

- Roles: Fishermen and laborers (e.g., Gorim Saltforge’s cousin, Thalia Veyl’s brother), drawn by promises of wealth.
- Motivation: Escape poverty and gain status.
- Stats: AC 12 (padded), HP 9 (2d8), Speed 30 ft. Skills: Athletics +2. Equipment: Club, robes.

6-9. Shadow Acolytes (3 Male/Female Humans, 18-30, Level 1 Cultists)

- Roles: Young villagers (e.g., Tobin Gale’s friend, Lina Drift’s sibling), lured by curiosity and power fantasies.
- Motivation: Prove themselves against elders’ authority.
- Stats: As above, with Stealth +2.

10. Veyra “Whisperclaw” Kren (Female Half-Elf, 28, Level 1 Rogue)

- Role: Scout and enforcer, a smuggler’s daughter with a knack for stealth.
- Motivation: Ambition to rival Kael Draven’s crew.
- Stats: AC 13 (leather), HP 7 (1d8), Speed 30 ft. Skills: Stealth +4, Deception +3. Equipment: Shortsword, dagger.

Cult Lair: The Bloodgrove

- Layout: A circular grove with 6 key areas, connected by narrow paths.
    1. Outer Watch: 2 Cultists guard the entrance (50 XP).
    2. Rune Circle: Carved stones with a faint red glow (DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) to detect ward drain).
    3. Ritual Pit: 2 Cultists and Veyra prepare a sacrifice (100 XP), pit trap (DC 12 Dexterity save, 1d6 bludgeoning).
    4. Altar Chamber: Korvax leads 3 Cultists in a ritual (150 XP), altar holds a Demon’s Eye amulet (detects evil, 25 gp).
    5. Storage Nook: Supplies and a letter from “Z” (Zarathrax, via Retch) (DC 14 Investigation).
    6. Escape Tunnel: Exit guarded by 1 Cultist (50 XP), collapses if triggered (DC 13 Dexterity save).
- XP Total: ~350 XP (watch 50, pit 100, chamber 150, tunnel 50), plus 200 XP for Korvax (CR 1), totaling ~550 XP. Add 200 XP for roleplay/negotiation, reaching 750-800 XP, split ~150-160 XP per player.

Zarathrax the Bloodwhisper

- Description: A minor demon lord of ambition and blood, offering power via pacts. His influence is weak but growing, tied to the hearthstone’s strain.
- Effect: Cult rituals drain 1d4 ward strength (DM discretion), summoning a Quasit (CR 1) if uninterrupted for 1 hour.

> [!NOTE]
> Campaign Integration
> 
> - The Auror’s Bounty: The cult seeks the pearl’s power, clashing with Retch and Carival’s plans.
> - Rune Connection: Their rune circle mimics the markers, suggesting a corrupted ward ritual.
> - Hearthstone’s Secret: Draining the ward weakens Brinehaven’s defense, linking to Ysmeine’s flute.
> - Carival and Smugglers: Retch may fund the cult, using Zarathrax to rival Carival.
> - Sahuagin Threat: The priestess opposes the cult, fearing demonic rivalry.

> [!warning]
> Interaction
> 
> - Encounter: The party hears chants 4 miles northeast. Entering, they face Korvax at the altar.
> - Dialogue (Korvax):  
>     Korvax: (raising a dagger, voice hoarse)  
>     "Outsiders! You tread on sacred ground! Zarathrax promises power—join us, or bleed for it. The village’s light fades, and we’ll rise! What say you?"
> - Options:
>     - Persuasion (DC 14): Offer an alternative (e.g., expose Drenvar). Success disbands the cult; failure triggers combat.
>     - Intimidation (DC 15): Threaten destruction. Success scatters them; failure summons the Quasit.
>     - Insight (DC 13): Detect desperation. Success reveals their motives; failure needs combat.
> - Quest: Stop the ritual (30 gp from Mirra Gale, 200 XP), preventing ward drain.
