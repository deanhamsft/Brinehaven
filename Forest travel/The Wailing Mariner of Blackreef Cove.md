As told by [[Lirien Thaloryn]]

Gather close, travelers, and let the fire’s glow guide your ears to an old Brinehaven legend—the Wailing Mariner. They say, nigh on three hundred years past, when the village was young and the sea wilder, a ship called the Dawn’s Lament set sail from these shores. Her captain, a stern man named Veyrin Saltspire, was bound for the imperial capital with a cargo of gold and a cursed relic—a black pearl plucked from an elven shrine. But the sea turned against him, and a tempest cast the ship onto the reefs of Blackreef Cove, where she splintered and sank with all hands.The villagers heard Veyrin’s cries that night, a mournful wail carried on the wind, blaming the pearl for his doom. Some say his spirit lingers still, bound to the cove, his ghostly form a flicker of lantern light amid the waves. Fishermen swear they’ve seen him pacing the shore, his tattered coat dripping seawater, searching for the pearl that cursed him. Others claim his wails grow louder when the hearthstone dims, as if the village’s ancient ward holds him at bay—yet barely.The elders whisper that Veyrin’s ghost guards the wreck, now known as the Auror’s Bounty, and that only returning the pearl to its shrine will free him. But beware—those who seek him often vanish, their boats found adrift with claw marks from sahuagin, drawn by the curse’s echo. On nights like this, with the moon thin and the tide high, listen close—you might hear his lament, a warning or a plea.

Setting the Scene, the Brinehaven Hearth is alive with the crackle of fire and the murmur of villagers. The storm has passed, leaving a chill wind that carries a faint, eerie moan from the coast. Calen Shorewhistle strums a mournful tune, while Elda Driftwood, nursing an ale, leans forward to spin the tale. The party’s presence—perhaps discussing recent finds like the reagent or the children’s crate—prompts her to share:Elda: (rasping, eyes glinting)  
"Ye lot, with yer wreck-chasin’ and rune-proddin’, ye oughta know the Wailin’ Mariner’s story. Veyrin Saltspire, he was—lost his ship to that cursed pearl three centuries back. I’ve seen his light myself, dancin’ over Blackreef Cove on nights like this. They say he’ll take ye if ye get too close, but some reckon he’s waitin’ for the pearl’s return. Heard it meself last week—louder than ever. What d’ye make of that?"Interaction Opportunities

- Investigation (DC 14 Intelligence): Piecing together Elda’s tale with party knowledge (e.g., the pearl, sahuagin) reveals Veyrin as Eryndor, earning 50 XP.
- Persuasion (DC 13 Charisma): Convince Elda for more details. Success yields a rumor of a “scarred man” (Retch) asking about the cove, tying to the smugglers.
- Performance (DC 12 Charisma): Recite or sing a version of the tale. Success impresses the crowd, granting inspiration.
- Quest Hook: Elda suggests investigating Blackreef Cove at night (25 gp, 1 sahuagin scout, DC 14 Wisdom (Perception)), promising Veyrin’s location.
