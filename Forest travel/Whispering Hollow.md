Location and Description

- Distance and Direction: 2 miles south of Brinehaven, along a rocky coastal path through sparse pines and jagged cliffs. The journey takes 1-1.5 hours on foot (DC 12 Constitution save or 1 level of exhaustion) or 30-45 minutes on horseback from Woodcock Stables (40 gp rental).
- Geography: **A narrow, crescent-shaped cove with a pebbled beach framed by sheer cliffs. The water is unnaturally still, reflecting a pale, ghostly light, and the air carries a low, mournful whisper. Sunken ruins—part of an ancient shipwreck—peek above the water at low tide, surrounded by swirling mist. A faint elven rune glows on a cliff face, hinting at a ward.**
- Atmosphere: Eerie and melancholic, with the whispers intensifying at night, accompanied by cold breezes. The site feels watched, its treasure guarded by a restless spirit.

Inhabitants
[[Specter.png]]
[[Water Weird.png]]
- Haunting Spirit: Lysara the Lamenter (use Specter stats, CR 1, Monster Manual p. 279, with +10 HP and a custom ability), the ghost of an elven sailor betrayed and drowned 400 years ago. She seeks her lost locket, believing it holds her soul’s peace.
- Minions: 2 Water Weird (CR 3, Monster Manual p. 299, scaled to CR 1 with 22 HP each), animated by Lysara’s sorrow to guard the treasure.
- Wildlife: Glowing crabs (non-hostile, 1d4 hp if eaten) scuttle among the ruins.

Key Features

- Sunken Ruins: The remains of a small elven vessel, submerged 10-15 ft deep. A DC 12 Strength (Athletics) check (or Water Breathing) is needed to search, revealing a chest (DC 13 Dexterity (Thieves’ Tools) to unlock).
- Rune-Cliff: A glowing elven rune (DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana)) ties to the hearthstone ward, weakening Lysara’s hold if activated with a song (e.g., Ysmeine’s flute, DC 14 Charisma (Performance)).
- Whispering Mist: A magical effect (DC 13 Wisdom save or disadvantage on Perception for 1 minute) that mimics Lysara’s voice, pleading for help or cursing intruders.
- Treasure Cache: The chest contains 50 gp, a Silver Locket (nonmagical, sentimental to Lysara, 25 gp), and a Potion of Healing.

Haunted Lore

The tale of Lysara the Lamenter is whispered by Brinehaven’s elders, especially Elda Driftwood. Four centuries ago, Lysara, an elven navigator, sailed with a crew seeking a new home. Betrayed by her human captain, who coveted her silver locket (a family heirloom), she was cast overboard during a storm. Her spirit now haunts Whispering Hollow, guarding the ship’s sunken gold and cursing those who take it without returning her locket to the sea. Locals avoid the cove, claiming her wails signal sahuagin attacks, though some believe freeing her could strengthen the village’s ward.Campaign Integration

- The Auror’s Bounty: Lysara’s wreck predates the Auror’s Bounty, but her locket’s magic may hint at the pearl’s origin, tying to Captain Eryndor’s curse.
- Rune Connection: The cliff rune links to the coastal markers, suggesting an elven network Lysara’s spirit protects, weakened by her unrest.
- Hearthstone’s Secret: Freeing Lysara could reinforce the ward, aligning with the hearthstone’s glow and Ysmeine’s ritual.
- Carival and Smugglers: Retch or Carival may seek the locket’s rumored power, clashing with the party.
- Sahuagin Threat: Lysara’s wails attract sahuagin, explaining their presence near Brinehaven.

Interaction with the Party

- Encounter: When the part arrives, they face Lysara’s mist, her spectral form rising from the water.
- Dialogue (Lysara):  
    Lysara: (voice echoing, mournful)  
    **"Intruders… my gold, my curse. Return my locket, stolen by treachery, and I’ll rest. Take it, and my wrath will drown you. Choose wisely…"**
- Handling Responses:
    - Persuasion (DC 13 Charisma): Offer to find the locket. Success calms her; failure triggers water weirds.
    - Intimidation (DC 15 Charisma): Threaten her spirit. Success forces retreat; failure summons both weirds.
    - Insight (DC 14 Wisdom): Detect her sorrow. Success reveals the locket’s location (chest); failure needs combat.
- Quest: Retrieve and return the locket to free Lysara (20 gp from Elda, 150 XP).

Quest: Free Lysara the Lamenter

- Objective: Locate the locket in the chest, return it to the sea.
- Challenge:
    - Swim: DC 12 Strength (Athletics) to reach the ruins, DC 13 Dexterity (Thieves’ Tools) to unlock.
    - Combat: 2 water weirds (200 XP total) if Lysara is angered, DC 13 Wisdom save vs. Life Drain (1d8 necrotic).
    - Ritual: DC 14 Charisma (Performance) to sing the locket into the sea, freeing her.
- Reward: 20 gp, Lysara’s gratitude (ward boost, +1 to saves vs. fear for 24 hours), 150 XP.
- Consequence: Success strengthens the ward; failure attracts 1d4 sahuagin next visit.
