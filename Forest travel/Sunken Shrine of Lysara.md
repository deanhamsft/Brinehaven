Overview

- Location: 20-30 feet underwater in Whispering Hollow, 2 miles south of Brinehaven, accessible via the cove’s still waters. The shrine is part of Lysara’s sunken elven vessel, visible at low tide or with Water Breathing. The journey takes 1-1.5 hours on foot to the cove, plus diving time.
- Theme: A sacred yet melancholic elven sanctuary, corrupted by Lysara’s unrest and the black tarry reagent, guarded by her spirit and aquatic threats.
- Level Suitability: Designed for a Level 3-4 party (CR 1 to 2 encounters, totaling 800-1,000 XP for 4-6 players, split ~160-250 XP each).
- Time: Exploration takes 1.5-2 hours.

Shrine Layout

The shrine consists of three chambers, connected by flooded corridors (10 ft wide, 20-30 ft deep, requiring swim speed or Water Breathing). Each chamber is 20x20 ft, with 15-ft-high ceilings of coral-encrusted stone.

Chamber 1: **Submerged Vestibule**

- Description: A cracked archway with faded elven runes opens into the shrine, surrounded by swirling mist and glowing seaweed. The water carries a faint, mournful hum.
- Inhabitants: 2 Merrow (CR 1, Monster Manual p. 219), aquatic guards corrupted by Lysara’s sorrow.
- Features: A current trap (DC 13 Strength (Athletics) or pushed 10 ft, 1d6 bludgeoning from rocks).
- Challenge: Combat (200 XP total) or Stealth (DC 13) to bypass. Success reveals a safe path forward.
- Loot: 10 gp in a coral pouch.

Chamber 2: **Ritual Chamber**

- Description: A central chamber with a coral altar bearing a cracked statue of Lysara, stained with black tarry residue. A ritual pool glows red, pulsing with her unrest.
- Inhabitants: Lysara the Lamenter (use Specter stats, CR 1, Monster Manual p. 279, with +10 HP and Wail of Sorrow: DC 13 Wisdom save or 2d8 psychic damage, recharge 5-6), accompanied by 1 Water Weird (CR 3, scaled to CR 1 with 22 HP), animated by the reagent.
- Features: The pool (DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana)) can be purified with a song (DC 14 Charisma (Performance)), reducing Lysara’s HP by half. The altar hides a compartment (DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)) with a Pearl of Power fragment.
- Challenge: Combat (500 XP total) or Ritual (song weakens foes). Lysara’s Life Drain (1d8 necrotic) and the weird’s flow pose risks.
- Loot: Pearl of Power fragment (restores 1 spell level, 1/long rest), silver locket (25 gp, her heirloom), 20 gp in a clamshell.

Chamber 3: **Sanctum of Light**

- Description: A serene chamber with a statue of Aerdrie Faenya, its wings cracked but emitting a faint glow. The air feels lighter, hinting at redemption.
- Inhabitants: None, but a Guardian Naga (CR 5, scaled to CR 2 with 40 HP) appears if the shrine is desecrated (e.g., taking all treasure without freeing Lysara).
- Features: The statue (DC 14 Wisdom (Religion)) offers a Bless effect (1 hour) if honored with the locket. A rusted chest (DC 13 Dexterity (Thieves’ Tools)) holds additional loot.
- Challenge: Roleplay (return the locket, DC 14 Charisma (Performance) to free Lysara) or Combat (400 XP if triggered). Success restores peace; failure risks the naga.
- Loot: 40 gp, Potion of Water Breathing, elven dagger (+1 to attack, 1d4+1 piercing, 10 gp) in the chest.

Dungeon Flow

- Entry (Chamber 1): The party faces merrow, setting a combat or stealth tone.
- Progression (Chamber 2): The ritual chamber offers a key fight and puzzle, building tension.
- Climax (Chamber 3): The sanctum presents a moral choice (free Lysara or loot) and a potential boss, concluding the dive.
- Exit: The shrine floods post-ritual (DC 12 Strength (Athletics) to swim out), urging haste.

Campaign Integration

- The Auror’s Bounty: The shrine’s elven origin parallels the Auror’s Bounty’s pearl, with Lysara’s locket hinting at Eryndor’s curse.
- Rune Connection: The vestibule runes tie to the coastal markers, reinforcing the ward network.
- Hearthstone’s Secret: Freeing Lysara strengthens the hearthstone, aligning with Ysmeine’s flute ritual.
- Carival and Smugglers: Retch or Carival may seek the shrine’s relics, clashing with the party.
- Sahuagin Threat: The merrow indicate sahuagin ties, linking to the priestess.

Interaction with the Party

- Encounter: The party dives into Whispering Hollow after Elda’s tale. Lysara’s mist rises, her voice echoing.
- Dialogue (Lysara):  
    Lysara: (mournful wail)  
    "Seekers… my shrine, my prison. Return my locket, stolen by betrayal, and I’ll rest. Take my gold, and my wrath will bind you to this deep. Choose…”
- Options:
    - Persuasion (DC 13 Charisma): Promise to free her. Success calms her; failure triggers combat.
    - Intimidation (DC 15 Charisma): Demand submission. Success forces retreat; failure summons weird and naga.
    - Insight (DC 14 Wisdom): Sense her plea. Success locates the locket; failure needs search.
- Quest: Free Lysara by returning the locket (20 gp from Elda, 200 XP).

DM Notes

- XP Total: ~1,100 XP (merrow 200, Lysara/weird 500, naga 400), split ~220 XP per player, suitable for Level 3-4.
- Scaling: For Level 2, reduce to 1 merrow and no naga. For Level 5, add a Ghast (CR 2).
- Treasure: 70 gp and items support Level 3-4 progression, with lore as a key reward.
